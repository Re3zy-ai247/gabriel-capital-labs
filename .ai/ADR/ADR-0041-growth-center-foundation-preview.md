# ADR-0041: Growth Center Foundation Preview

Status: **ACCEPTED — GROWTH EXPERIENCE PHASE 1A APPROVED FOUNDER PREVIEW ONLY**
Date: 2026-07-31
Decision owner: Founder

## Context

The Founder authorized a protected, synthetic, non-monetary Growth Center experience for review. That approval applies to the Phase 1A preview boundary, not a participant program or later contract.

## Decision

Provide one direct-link Founder-review route with fixed synthetic districts and deterministic explanations. The route is subordinate to the production-hard-off Founder-review policy and one exact Growth Center preview gate. It is absent from product navigation and public discovery.

Growth owns route-local wording, fixtures, and presentation only. CXOS Core Runtime supplies headless lifecycle mechanics. No live Growth bounded context, participant record, runtime model, owner-system read, reputation effect, Community or Marketplace mutation, billing, payout, or economics exists.

## Approval boundary

Phase 1A approval does not approve the Phase 1B contract, source integration, schema, participant access, public access, production activation, or a later gate.

**GROWTH EXPERIENCE PHASE 1A — APPROVED FOUNDER PREVIEW ONLY.**
