CreditVector Growth Network — Production Roadmap#

Status: FOUNDATION SLICE ONLY · ALL ACTIVATION PHASES OWNER-GATED Version: 1.0 Date: 2026-07-31 Architecture: GROWTH_NETWORK_ARCHITECTURE.md Product acceptance: GROWTH_NETWORK_PRODUCT_SPEC.md

This is a dependency-ordered roadmap, not authorization to continue. The current stream stops after Phase 0. No production payouts, tax collection, billing changes, schema changes, or protected-surface modifications are part of this delivery.
Growth Experience status addendum (2026-07-31): GROWTH EXPERIENCE PHASE 1A — APPROVED FOUNDER PREVIEW; GROWTH EXPERIENCE PHASE 1B — AUTHORIZED NON-MONETARY CONTRACT WORK; CGN ECONOMIC PHASE 1A — BLOCKED. The protected synthetic experience work in ADR-0041 and ADR-0042 does not activate a participant program or change this roadmap’s economic, identity, policy, schema, or specialist gates. Earlier “no UI/route/consumer” statements describe the dormant economic/program foundation.

1. Roadmap laws#

  1. Architecture and ownership before schema.
  2. Identity and organization authorization before any organization-scoped record.
  3. Authoritative source facts before events and projections.
  4. Policy simulation before payable amounts.
  5. Shadow accounting before provider design.
  6. Counsel/accountant approval before compensation or tax implementation.
  7. Human-reviewed pilot before automation.
  8. Reconciliation proof before scale.
  9. Every phase remains fail-closed and independently reversible.
  10. Mission Control, Agency Command, Arena, CXOS rooms, and Community remain out of scope.

4. Phase 0.5 — Founder Economic Decision Gate#

Status: REQUIRED NEXT · NO CODE · NOT YET PASSED.

Goal#

Turn the north-star architecture into one testable economic wedge before building platform machinery.

Founder decisions#

  1. Select the first shadow wedge. Recommendation: Agency Builder shadow qualification with one invited design-partner cohort and one or two lawful organization-quality evidence dimensions.
  2. Define the economic nature of Agency Growth Distribution: discretionary funded pool, contractual revenue share, direct service compensation, rebate, or another counsel/accountant-approved structure.
  3. Set proposed funding source, maximum cycle liability, reserve/solvency rule, no-cross-subsidy rule, and GCL value capture. New participant money or required purchases cannot fund earlier participants.
  4. Choose path, participant class, pilot jurisdiction, legal payor, authoritative source evidence, and manual-review/appeal operator.
  5. Approve policy RACI across Founder, CCO, Finance/Controller, Engineering, Integrity, and Appeals.
  6. Decide how verified active status is used. Current compliance proposal: it validates only an actor's own evidence; operator count/addition does not affect eligibility or allocation.
  7. Decide whether one-level affiliate attribution and free-standing earning eligibility are permanent policy or counsel-driven pilot constraints.
  8. Define shadow success/kill thresholds, including participant value, GCL value capture, support/review cost, concentration, complaints, reversals, and recruiting correlation.
  9. Confirm that the 15th applies to Agency Growth Distribution only unless expressly extended.
  10. Approve the rule that shadow records can never be promoted into legal obligations.

Exit evidence#

  • signed Founder decision memo and updated ADR-0039 status;
  • CCO/counsel issue list and assigned specialists;
  • one-page wedge spec with explicit non-goals;
  • operating owner and budget for concierge review/support;
  • no code, schema, event, or public claim merely from passing this gate.

8. Phase 3 — Selected evidence wedge and qualification shadow run#

Status: PROPOSED; depends on Phases 1–2 and source owners.

Goal#

Prove one Founder-selected non-monetary evidence wedge on historical/synthetic or explicitly approved own-data without creating obligations. Do not integrate every source context at once.

Work#

  • Source-owner evidence contract(s) only for the selected one- or two-dimension wedge. Other candidate sources—retention/health, onboarding, Reputation contribution, education, mentorship, complaints/reversals—remain future adapters.
  • Growth Evidence Qualification decisions: received, pending, accepted, matured, held, rejected, invalidated.
  • Sealed qualification cycles and source watermarks.
  • Growth Reputation View as a private rebuildable projection.
  • Shadow cycle reports with reason/evidence/policy provenance.

Hard rules#

  • Operator-related evidence is admissible only when its actor was independently verified and active during the evidence window. Operator count/addition cannot unlock eligibility or affect allocation.
  • No XP/cash conversion.
  • No browser-supplied verification or amount.
  • No speculative Event Fabric contracts before each authoritative producer exists.
  • No public comparison or earnings output.
  • Shadow records use a physically and type-isolated SHADOW namespace/store with no transition, copy, promotion, or posting path into live qualification, obligations, or settlement.

Exit evidence#

  • deterministic replay equals original decisions;
  • missing/late/invalid/reversed evidence tests;
  • cohort fairness and sandbagging red team;
  • privacy/consent/k-anonymity review;
  • shadow decisions create zero economic journal entries.

13. Phase 8 — Internal shadow pilot#

Status: PROPOSED.

Goal#

Run complete cycles with synthetic or explicitly approved internal data and no legal obligation or money movement.

Pilot design#

  1. Start with a small, diverse, fixed cohort and published shadow rules.
  2. Run at least three full monthly cycles including one reversal/dispute scenario.
  3. Compare deterministic decisions against independent finance/CCO review.
  4. Measure fairness across agency size, tenure, path, and eligible cohorts.
  5. Red-team recruiting incentives, self-funding, collusion, sandbagging, false evidence, insider override, and data leakage.
  6. Exercise appeal, incident, rollback, replay, and statement reconstruction.
  7. Do not show participants prospective earnings or imply payment.
  8. Operate concierge-style with named design partners, scheduled interviews, documented support time, evidence-correction logs, and weekly Founder/CCO review.
  9. Test the cold start explicitly: can a new participant find a first useful action and produce first verified value without recruiting or an existing audience?

Exit thresholds#

  • zero duplicate journal postings;
  • zero cross-tenant authorization defects;
  • zero unexplained monetary differences;
  • all decisions reproducible from sealed inputs/policy;
  • all holds/reversals/appeals traceable;
  • false-positive and cohort-impact results accepted by CCO/Founder;
  • operational staff can execute cycle without engineer intervention;
  • final compensation, tax, employment, privacy, payments, accounting, and marketing signoffs complete.
  • participant adoption, repeat verified value, support/review cost, and GCL value-capture hypothesis meet the Phase 0.5 thresholds.

16. Launch strategy#

Stage A — Foundation communication#

Internal only. Describe CGn as future participation and accounting architecture. Do not announce income, rates, eligibility, or launch date.

Stage B — Shadow qualification#

Invitation-only, no money, no public claims. Participants can inspect evidence and reason codes and report errors.

Stage C — Shadow statements#

Clearly labeled simulations with no obligation or payment promise. Run appeals and corrections.

Stage D — Controlled live pilot#

Only after Phase 9 approval. One jurisdiction/participant class/revenue stream where possible, conservative caps/reserves, manual maker-checker, daily reconciliation, immediate kill switch.

Stage E — Graduated expansion#

Increase cohorts/streams only when fraud, complaints, reversals, reconciliation, support, fairness, and unit economics remain within owner-approved thresholds.

Stage F — General availability#

Requires stable operations, audited controls, substantiated communications, tax/payment reliability, participant support, transparent policies, and documented board/founder risk acceptance.

19. Next recommended implementation#

After this foundation, the next step is Phase 0.5’s Founder Economic Decision Gate, not engineering. Select the wedge and business/economic controls first.

If the Founder selects the recommended organization-scoped Agency Builder wedge, the likely next economic engineering slice is CGN Economic Phase 1B's Organization Event Fabric correction. It must be a narrowly reviewed platform prerequisite, not a Growth feature and not a reinterpretation of legacy agencyId. If the Founder selects an own-operator Path B wedge, complete only the CGN Economic Phase 1A shared prerequisites it actually needs.

In parallel, the business team should commission the compensation-plan/CROA, tax, worker-classification, privacy/Safeguards, payments/custody, and accounting decision matrices. Code should not guess those answers.
