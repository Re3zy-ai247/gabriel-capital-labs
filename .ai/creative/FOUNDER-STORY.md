## The five pillars
1. **The problem is asymmetry, not credit.** Bureaus, furnishers, and collectors run on systems, data, and law. The consumer gets a PDF and a prayer. 3. **Why education matters.** The industry sells dependency ("give us $99/mo forever"). GCL sells capability: understand your report, know your rights (FCRA §611, §605, §623 — real statutes, plain English), run your own process. Software and education first — a user who outgrows us is a success story we'll tell.
4. **Why compliance matters.** Every "guaranteed deletion" ad is a future FTC case and a present betrayal. We made the constraint the identity: a compliance scrubber in the codebase, human gates on every publish, and one promise — the process, never the outcome. In a category built on overpromising, restraint is the moat.
5. **Why trust us.** Receipts, everywhere: every Kai answer shows its sources; every metric on our dashboards is real or labeled "not yet instrumented"; every letter cites the actual statute. We built the company so it can't lie casually — then we tell people exactly that.
