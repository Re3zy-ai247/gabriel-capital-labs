## Founder summary

CreditVector can support a durable professional economy without becoming an affiliate plugin, recruiting program, or gamified hierarchy. This proposed foundation preserves two founder-directed paths without ranking them or implying either program is active:

- **Agency Builder:** qualify through the verified health and success of an organization—not the number of people recruited.
- **Professional Operator:** a future path for building an independent business or career through verified education, mentorship, creation, consulting, and a proposed responsibly governed affiliate program.

No participant-facing CGn program is active, no person is presently eligible under CGn, and no payment is promised.

The architecture makes six decisive choices:

1. **No recruitment economics.** Invitations, signups, headcount, participant purchases, paid retention, downlines, and downstream overrides cannot increase compensation or unlock eligibility. The current **proposed compliance implementation** treats “verified active operator” only as an admissibility condition on that actor’s own evidence: the actor must be independently verified and active during the evidence window, while the number or addition of operators does not affect eligibility or allocation. Founder/counsel must ratify this mechanism or an equally non-recruiting alternative at Phase 0.5.
2. **One truth owner.** CGn reuses Identity, Organizations, Membership, Reputation, Performance Intelligence, Entitlements, Event Fabric, Billing, Analytics, and Kai. It does not fork them.
3. **Growth Reputation is a view, not a second score.** It privately presents mentoring, onboarding, success, education, retention, contribution, and ecosystem-health evidence from canonical owners. It creates no XP, rating, leaderboard, or cash conversion.
4. **Qualification, accounting, and payment are separate.** A qualified participant is not necessarily accrued, payable, scheduled, or paid. An immutable economic journal is the future source of obligations; only a reconciled provider receipt can prove payment.
5. **The 15th is predictable but honest.** Agency Growth Distribution is scheduled for the 15th. Other revenue streams keep their source/contract cadence unless the Founder explicitly extends the rule. Qualification window, verification days, weekend/holiday settlement, amounts, rates, caps, tax treatment, and provider mechanics remain unratified.
6. **The code is inert.** The first package has an exact-string off-by-default flag, a hard-false payout sentinel, pure constants/date validation, zero consumers, and a guard rejecting persistence, payments, providers, protected surfaces, and speculative economic values.

The next step is not engineering or payouts. It is Phase 0.5: the Founder selects one shadow wedge and decides its economic nature, funding/liability limits, value capture, governance, cohort, and success/kill criteria. Organization-scoped Event Fabric work becomes a conditional prerequisite only if the selected Path A wedge needs it. Business work should proceed in parallel on compensation/CROA, tax, worker classification, privacy/Safeguards, payments/custody, and accounting decision matrices.

## 1. Mission

CGn is a proposed governed economic layer intended, after separate approvals, to let CreditVector participants:

- build successful organizations;
- establish independent professional careers;
- educate and mentor;
- create and sell useful assets/services;
- contribute evidence-backed value;
- collaborate inside accountable organizations;
- receive transparent recognition and, only after separate approvals, amounts arising from their own accepted services or an approved organization-quality program.

It optimizes for compliance, trust, retention, education, mentorship, evidence, ecosystem health, participant dignity, and long-term business durability.

### 1.1 Proposed economic thesis

CGn’s durable advantage is not the flag, dashboard, referral code, or ledger. It is a **verified professional-value graph**:

```text
source-owned evidence
  → explainable qualification
  → targeted education, mentorship, and Kai preparation
  → stronger operator/organization outcomes
  → better verified evidence
  → trusted discovery and economic opportunity
```

Participant value is better capability, proof of contribution, trusted discovery, direct sales/services, and a possible Agency Growth Distribution. Proposed GCL value capture is higher legitimate subscription retention/expansion plus future marketplace, program, and enterprise fees—not recruitment or participant entry fees.

The economic nature and funding of Agency Growth Distribution remain a **Founder decision**: discretionary funded pool, contractual revenue share, direct service compensation, rebate, or another counsel/accountant-approved structure. Before any economic code, the Founder/Finance/CCO must set the funding source, cycle budget, maximum liability, reserve/solvency rule, GCL value capture, and no-cross-subsidy rule. New participant money or required purchases may never fund an earlier participant’s return.

## 4. Economic paths

### Path A — Agency Builder

An Agency Builder establishes a separately accepted program relationship linked to:

- active Operator Identity;
- active Organization;
- active owner Membership;
- explicit economic beneficiary/payee designation;
- future compliance/tax readiness.

Agency Growth Distribution may eventually consider retention quality, onboarding outcomes, mentorship, education, canonical contribution, and ecosystem integrity. Operator-related evidence is admissible only when its actor was independently verified and active during the relevant window. The formula must not use operator count or addition, subscriptions, purchases, recruits, consumer payment duration, credit outcomes, or dispute volume as an eligibility threshold or compensation input.

Organization ownership grants no automatic right to another person’s earnings. Membership is operational authority, not economic ownership.

### Path B — Professional Operator

A future Professional Operator path would not require an organization and may support value creation as:

- educator;
- mentor;
- marketplace creator;
- community expert through verified contribution—not popularity;
- responsible affiliate growth partner; one-level attribution is a **proposed compliance constraint**, not yet ratified;
- Kai workflow/asset creator;
- consultant, speaker, or trainer;
- future sponsor, partner, or enterprise-program provider.

Each revenue stream requires its own source evidence, agreement, compliance, dispute, tax, and accounting semantics. The current compliance proposal forbids any purchase/subscription from itself generating or increasing compensation or being bought solely to qualify. Founder/counsel must decide whether legitimate paid platform access can coexist with separately earned eligibility; recruitment, inventory buying, or agency ownership cannot be required.

## 7. Monthly Growth Distribution constitution

- **Ratified:** Agency Growth Distribution is scheduled for the 15th of every month. This does not set the cadence for marketplace, course, service, affiliate, sponsorship, partnership, or enterprise proceeds.
- **Proposed:** previous calendar month as observation window; 1st–10th verification/reconciliation; 11th–14th review/lock.
- **Needs confirmation:** weekend/holiday treatment and the distinction between scheduled date, provider acceptance, and bank settlement.
- **Unratified:** eligible evidence set, formula, allocation pool, weights, caps, minimums, reserves, rounding, currency, tax classification, clawbacks, offsets, jurisdictions, providers, and activation.

The future cycle must preserve:

```text
source-owned evidence
  → sealed qualification snapshot
  → reason-coded decision
  → integrity/compliance/tax/finance controls
  → immutable obligation journal
  → future settlement intent
  → future provider receipt
```

No earlier state can be displayed as “paid.” Late evidence never silently changes a locked statement.

## 8. Team Operations constitution

Team Operations is an accountable organization workspace for announcements, tasks, meeting briefs, shared-work references, training, mentorship coordination, and optional privacy-safe presence.

Permanent boundaries:

- Identity owns Organization and Membership.
- Agency Command retains existing agency campaign/task/priority truth; Operator Network/future Rooms retain messaging/channel truth; the scheduler/Meetings context retains calendar truth; canonical encrypted storage retains document bytes.
- Non-agency work coordination ownership is unresolved and requires an ADR before any schema. CGn must not create generic `Task`, `Channel`, or `Campaign` records.
- A future scheduler/calendar owns calendar truth.
- Canonical encrypted storage owns document bytes.
- Learning and Mentorship own their outcome facts.
- Kai prepares, summarizes, and recommends; a human remains owner/assignee/approver.
- General channels cannot become customer-credit-data stores.
- Presence is off by default, ephemeral, opt-in, and prohibited as a reputation, compensation, retention, or worker-performance input.
- No Team Operations implementation is part of this foundation.

## 9. Kai constitution inside CGn

Kai acts as a Growth Strategist and provides an executive-intelligence function by:

- preparing owner/operator/educator/mentor briefings;
- explaining decisions and missing evidence;
- recommending education, mentorship, onboarding, contribution, and organization-health actions;
- preparing campaigns, meetings, training, and drafts;
- coordinating read-only context across authorized owners.

Kai never owns or changes calendar, tasks, billing, organizations, identity, customers, tax, risk decisions, qualification, ledger, or settlement. It never initiates payout or makes legal/tax/earnings claims.

The global canonical Kai title remains **Credit Intelligence Officer** under `KAI-OS.md`. “Chief Executive Intelligence Officer” is recorded as the founder’s desired CGn functional framing and requires a separate founder-approved KAI-OS amendment before becoming the global title.

## 10. Architecture review findings

### Accepted design

- CGn belongs at CreditVector L4–L6 on the frozen L0–L3 spine.
- Bounded contexts are independent and connected through versioned refs-only facts.
- Experiences/projections own no truth.
- The economic ledger is append-only; reversals compensate.
- Policy is deterministic, versioned, replayable, evidence-backed, and appealable.
- The first slice is contract-only and completely dormant.

### Blocking platform prerequisites

1. **Organization-scoped Event Fabric:** the existing envelope’s `agencyId` belongs to the legacy consumer-management axis and cannot safely authorize generic `Organization.id`. No organization-scoped CGn event replay/read may activate until an additive reviewed scope exists.
2. **Identity activation:** Operator Identity, Organization, and Membership foundations are dormant; relevant lifecycle/migration/PEP stages must pass before CGn records use them.
3. **Performance Intelligence:** retention/health/onboarding evidence needs a canonical producer and complete provenance before qualification.
4. **Entitlements / Reward Claim:** challenges/rewards/marketplace unlocks remain deferred until that context exists.
5. **Source commerce contexts:** Marketplace, Learning, Mentorship, and services must own settled/completed/refund/dispute facts before revenue adapters.
6. **Event reliability:** Event Fabric is fact transport, not a monetary queue. Ledger/source transactions, idempotency, replay, and reconciliation are authoritative.

## 12. Anti-fraud review

Verdict: **Architecture supports explainable prevention; rules/thresholds remain unratified.**

### Covered threats

- fake/self/related-party referrals;
- duplicate and synthetic accounts;
- inactive/sham organizations and membership;
- circular payments and collusion;
- commission/refund/chargeback fraud;
- Sybil networks and credential sharing;
- contribution duplication, plagiarism, fabricated education/mentorship, low-effort flooding;
- retention manipulation and deliberate baseline degradation;
- reviewer conflict, manual-override abuse, and insider fraud.

### Mandatory behavior

Signal → case → hold where necessary → independent human review → reason-coded disposition → notice/appeal. A signal is not guilt; an AI score cannot make an adverse decision. TIN/W-9 data or derivatives are prohibited anti-fraud inputs.
