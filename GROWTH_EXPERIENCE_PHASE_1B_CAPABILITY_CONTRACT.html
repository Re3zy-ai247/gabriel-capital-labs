1. Purpose#

This contract gives the protected Growth Center Founder Preview a deterministic vocabulary for explaining how an operator could become more capable and useful through:

  • professional development;
  • mentorship preparation;
  • operator education;
  • useful creation;
  • Community contribution;
  • collaboration; and
  • responsible organization stewardship.

It defines presentation, ownership, evidence-requirement, review-state, privacy, correction, appeal, and failure behavior. It does not create a live Growth program or a new source of truth.

The product promise is:

Build capability people can trust.

It is not:

Recruit people or qualify for compensation.

2. Permanent product laws#

  1. Contribution is the focus; growth is the outcome.
  2. Recruiting is not rewarded.
  3. Capability is not a proxy for compensation, eligibility, rank, or reputation.
  4. Claimed success is not source-confirmed evidence.
  5. Completion is not approval, qualification, certification, publication, recognition, reputation, or economic eligibility.
  6. Growth Center may compose authorized references and project their meaning; it may not silently become their canonical owner.
  7. A missing, unknown, conflicting, or unauthorized source fails closed.
  8. Kai may prepare and explain fixed review fixtures; Kai may not decide, approve, verify, match, enroll, publish, schedule, adjudicate, or act.
  9. Privacy is deny-by-default. Visibility is explicit, scoped, revocable in future policy, and never inferred.
  10. Every correction challenges a source fact at its canonical owner. Every appeal challenges a review decision through an authorized human review path.
  11. No Phase 1B state produces money, a right to money, a balance, a qualification, or a promise of business or credit results.

6. Evidence-requirement contract#

Phase 1B uses fictional requirements and source categories only. It has no participant evidence record. Every supported contract projection carries one deeply frozen, owner-labeled, eleven-part evidence preview; the stage renders it only when the canonical owner is resolved.

A supported evidence-requirement preview contains exactly:

  1. Professional purpose — owned by the selected contract's canonical owner;
  2. Source owner — the future evidence owner, with an explicit no-connection statement;
  3. Subject and scope — a synthetic review scope with no person, participant, organization, or customer represented;
  4. Evidence category — a purpose-bound category, never a live artifact, submission, or verification;
  5. Occurrence / source confirmation — the future source-confirmation rule and refused substitutes;
  6. Reviewer role — the future authorized human review owner, or an unresolved-owner refusal;
  7. Policy / contract version — Growth synthetic review grammar version only; future source policy remains unconnected;
  8. Visibility — the contract's deny-by-default privacy boundary and visibility owner;
  9. Correction route — the source-fact owner and no-live-case boundary;
  10. Appeal route — the decision owner, or an unresolved independent-owner refusal; and
  11. Expiry / supersession — the future stale-state rule, with no live validity period.

For an owner-resolved contract, the stage displays these eleven items as definition-list rows with an explicit owner label. When the contract owner is unresolved, the projection maps all eleven items to the canonical unsupported copy and the stage withholds the detailed list. The preview never displays raw evidence content.

Evidence is purpose-bound. A source-confirmed-state example for one pathway cannot be reused for another purpose by inference. Self-attestation alone is never presented as source confirmation.

8. Correction and appeal contract#

A correction and an appeal are different:

  • Correction: challenges the accuracy, provenance, scope, or currency of a source fact. It routes conceptually to that fact's canonical owner.
  • Appeal: challenges a review disposition. It requires an underlying decision, a reason, and a future authorized human reviewer distinct from the original decision-maker where policy requires independence.

Growth Center may display Preview correction path or Preview appeal path. It may not display submit, file, open case, send, assign, schedule, or resolved as a live action. Nothing is stored or transmitted.

Source conflicts resolve to Needs human review. Kai and Growth never choose between conflicting sources.
