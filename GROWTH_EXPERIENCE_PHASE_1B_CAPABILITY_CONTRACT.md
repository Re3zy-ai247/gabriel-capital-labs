This contract gives the protected Growth Center Founder Preview a deterministic vocabulary for explaining how an operator could become more capable and useful through:

## 8. Correction and appeal contract

A correction and an appeal are different:

- **Correction:** challenges the accuracy, provenance, scope, or currency of a source fact. It routes conceptually to that fact's canonical owner.
- **Appeal:** challenges a review disposition. It requires an underlying decision, a reason, and a future authorized human reviewer distinct from the original decision-maker where policy requires independence.

Growth Center may display **Preview correction path** or **Preview appeal path**. It may not display submit, file, open case, send, assign, schedule, or resolved as a live action. Nothing is stored or transmitted.

Source conflicts resolve to **Needs human review**. Kai and Growth never choose between conflicting sources.
