# Cinematic Bible (moving-picture grammar — permanent)

Extends `CINEMATOGRAPHY.md` (still-render setups: INK/PAPER/AURORA lighting, camera locks, shot library) into film. Every GCL/CreditVector video must feel like the same universe: **the quiet operations room**.

## Camera language
- Locked-off or slow motivated moves only: 2–4s push-ins, lateral dollies tracking Kai's SHOT-F walk. NO handheld shake, NO whip-pans, NO crash zooms, NO drone swoops.
- Kai coverage obeys the Camera Bible (eye-level, 50mm portrait; 35mm for scene-wides). Rey coverage: chest-up interview 50mm, eye-level, honest — never hero-low.
- Screen/UI inserts are REAL captures (hybrid law), shot as slow push-ins on actual product; never mock UI.

## Color grading
- One LUT family across everything: cool navy shadows (ink family), neutral-true mids (skin + Kai's coat NEVER tinted — coat truth extends to grading), restrained teal accent highlights (rim lights, panel glows).
- Contrast: cinematic but readable; blacks hold detail (operations room, not a void). NO orange-teal blockbuster crush, NO desaturated misery, NO vintage film grain.

## Lighting (motion contexts)
INK STUDIO is the master world (per CINEMATOGRAPHY.md); Rey's interview setup mirrors Kai's: soft key upper-left, teal rim behind-right — the SAME light grammar on both characters is what makes them castmates. Daylight/location shoots (founder story content): natural light, graded into the LUT family — the universe tint follows Rey out of the studio.

## Lens & format
50mm primary / 35mm wide / 85mm for Rey emotional beats. Shallow-but-honest depth (subject sharp, background soft). Master in 16:9; compose center-safe for 9:16 crops (social derives, never reshoots). 24fps look for brand films; 30fps for product screencasts.

## Transitions & editing rhythm
- Cuts and simple 8-frame fades. The ONLY signature transition: **the projection wipe** — a Kai panel materializing carries us to the next scene (use ≤2 per video).
- Rhythm: calm base (3–6s shots) with ONE deliberate acceleration in the product-proof section (1–2s cuts as real UI performs), then settle again. The exhale-inhale-exhale shape is the house edit.
- Shorts compress the same shape: 1 calm second → fast middle → calm close on a face (Rey's or Kai's).

## Music & sound
- Palette: warm minimal electronic — soft synth pads, felt piano, low pulse; think "trust, at 90 BPM." NO epic trailer hits, NO corporate ukulele-whistle, NO meme audio under brand films (TikTok trends may be used ONLY in MK-T formats, and never under Kai's evidence beats).
- Kai is silent (no voice, no barks) in v1 canon: his "voice" is the projection sound — one soft materialize tone (glassy, brief) + sub-audible data ticks. If future voice is ever considered, that's a founder-level bible amendment (see CONTENT-PRODUCTION-PIPELINE §future stack).
- Rey's VO recorded dry and close; licensed music only (music licensing = existing CAMP-001 open item pattern).

## Motion & VFX
Projection/hologram grammar: `KAI-HOLOGRAM-SYSTEM.md`. Everything else obeys MOTION-BIBLE physics (slow-in/out, no bounce). VFX exist only to present evidence — if an effect doesn't carry data or continuity, cut it.

## The test
Before any video ships: *Does this feel like a quiet room where someone competent is already working?* If it feels loud, urgent, or salesy — it's off-universe. Recut.
