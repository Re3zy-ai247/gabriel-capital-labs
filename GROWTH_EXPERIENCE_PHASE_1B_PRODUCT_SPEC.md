Status: **GROWTH EXPERIENCE PHASE 1B — AUTHORIZED NON-MONETARY CONTRACT WORK**

## 1. Product decision

Build a nested Growth Center review experience that makes the future non-monetary capability model precise. A reviewer should understand:

> CreditVector can help an operator become more capable, useful, and trusted without turning contribution into recruiting, popularity, or a compensation promise.

Phase 1B defines how capability pathways, source ownership, evidence requirements, independent states, privacy, corrections, appeals, and Kai explanations could work. It does not enroll anyone or connect a canonical source.

## 3. Goals

- Make every supported capability pathway answer **How can I become more valuable?**
- Show that capability and contribution can support a strong operator business without requiring agency ownership.
- Define a legible contract between Growth presentation and canonical source owners.
- Demonstrate separate, correctable, reviewable state axes without implying live status.
- Show evidence requirements without collecting or inventing evidence.
- Explain privacy and visibility before any participant experiment.
- Let Kai explain fixed review fixtures while remaining deterministic and non-authoritative.
- Fail closed on every unsupported or ambiguous state.
- Preserve the approved Growth Center Phase 1A experience as the parent context.

## 9. Capability pathways

### 9.1 Professional capability

Question: **What should I practice next?**

Shows a synthetic capability definition and the source-owner contract a future system would need. It does not assess an operator.

### 9.2 Professional-development pathway

Question: **How do I become a stronger operator?**

Shows a learning-pathway preview for operations, compliance, accessibility, teaching, and platform use. Learning owns any future pathway, assessment, completion, credential, or certification. Phase 1B has no enrollment or outcome.

### 9.3 Mentorship preparation and participation

Question: **How should I prepare to learn from or support another operator?**

Shows goals, questions, boundaries, evidence categories, feedback preparation, and follow-through as future B2B operator professional development. Operator Network would own the relationship; Meetings would own a session; Documents would own notes or artifacts.

It offers no matching, screening, booking, contract, payment, live mentorship, consumer-specific credit work, dispute execution, representation, legal/tax/financial/employment/business-opportunity advice, advance-fee guidance, or promised result.

### 9.4 Educational program and learning artifact

Question: **What should I learn or teach?**

Shows outcome-led learning and teaching requirements. Learning owns a future program and completion; Documents owns artifacts. There is no course, class, webinar, instructor status, credential, certification, enrollment, or promised result.

### 9.5 Operator-created contribution

Question: **What useful work could I create?**

Shows requirements for a sourced template, operating note, educational outline, or reusable practice artifact. It does not create, upload, submit, publish, recognize, sell, or transfer anything.

### 9.6 Community contribution

Question: **Where could I share useful knowledge?**

Shows a synthetic path from useful idea to provenance and future Community review. Community owns submission, moderation, and publication. Growth does not read or write Community and does not derive status from posts, likes, views, followers, attendance, referrals, or recruiting.

### 9.7 Organizational stewardship

Question: **How could responsible leadership help an organization become healthier?**

Shows fictional stewardship requirements such as clear onboarding, useful mentoring, operational follow-through, and source-backed improvement. Organizations owns organizational truth; Membership owns authority; Performance Intelligence owns future organization-health and improvement projections; operational facts remain with their source owners.

Always display:

> **No live Growth Distribution. These stewardship examples create no eligibility, allocation, obligation, or payment right. Recruiting is not rewarded.**

## 10. State-axis experience

The reviewer can inspect eight visibly separate state lanes:

| Axis | Product question | Must not imply |
|---|---|---|
| Availability / contract support | Can this internal review concept be projected with a resolved owner? | Participation or approval |
| Preparation/participation | What fictional preparation or participation state is illustrated? | Completion, competence, employment |
| Evidence sufficiency | Are named fictional requirements present for one purpose? | Verification, acceptance, reuse |
| Completion | What fictional source-completion state is illustrated? | Review, credential, qualification, reputation |
| Source review | What fictional source-review disposition is illustrated? | Qualification, credential, reputation |
| Correction | How could a source fact be challenged? | Live filing or immediate fact change |
| Appeal | How could a review decision be challenged? | Live case, automatic reversal, Kai judgment |
| Visibility | Who could see the future source-owned fact under explicit policy? | Publication, consent, broad access |

Required non-inference copy:

> Completion is not approval, qualification, certification, publication, recognition, reputation, access, employment, or economic eligibility. Each state lane stands alone.

The UI must not use **verified**, **approved**, **qualified**, **certified**, **recognized**, **expert**, **verified mentor**, or **approved instructor** as a fixture status.

## 11. Synthetic fixtures

The review controls select one explicit allowlisted pathway and one explicit allowlisted fixture. The exact ten implemented fixtures are:

| ID | Label | Implemented review purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `overview` | Contract anatomy | Orientation to ownership and state-lane separation. |
| `empty` | No source record | No source record and no favorable inference. |
| `preparing` | Preparation | Preparation separated from participation, completion, and review. |
| `completed-unreviewed` | Source completion / no review | Fictional completion without review or consequential meaning. |
| `in-review` | Human review | Fictional human review with no decision. |
| `changes-requested` | Correction needed | Fictional correction path without a live case. |
| `source-corrected` | Source corrected | A prior projection superseded without rewriting history. |
| `appeal-in-review` | Independent appeal review | Fictional appeal review distinct from source correction. |
| `privacy-restricted` | Privacy restricted | Only a permitted summary; restricted details are absent. |
| `unsupported` | Unsupported state | No status, eligibility, completion, review, or action inferred. |

Not every contract/fixture pair is valid. Mentorship refuses `completed-unreviewed` and `appeal-in-review`; organizational stewardship refuses `appeal-in-review`; operator-created contribution remains owner-unresolved and cannot project a favorable state. Unknown, duplicate, partial, malformed, or impossible selections also fail closed.

Required-evidence-missing, not-accepted, source-conflict, source-owner-review, and resolved-appeal terms remain conceptual future vocabulary unless one of the exact fixtures above represents them. The rejection-pattern panel is a separate fixed educational surface, not a selectable fixture, detector, accusation, or sanction.

The no-query state is a fixed orientation fixture. Browser back and forward reproduce the selected supported fixture. The page does not infer history, remember a reviewer, or store selection state.

Unknown, multiple, malformed, incomplete, or impossible selections render:

> **Unsupported review state · unavailable in this Preview. No status, eligibility, completion, review, or action has been inferred.**

Raw unsupported input is never echoed.

## 12. Evidence-requirement view

For every supported owner-resolved projection, the workbench now renders exactly eleven fictional, owner-labeled items:

1. **Professional purpose**;
2. **Source owner** and explicit no-connection statement;
3. **Subject and scope** with no person, participant, organization, or customer represented;
4. **Evidence category** with no artifact, submission, or verification;
5. **Occurrence / source confirmation** and refused substitutes;
6. **Reviewer role** or unresolved-owner refusal;
7. **Policy / contract version** for the Growth review grammar only;
8. **Visibility** and privacy boundary;
9. **Correction route** to the source-fact owner;
10. **Appeal route** to the decision owner or independent-owner refusal; and
11. **Expiry / supersession** with no live validity period.

Each rendered item displays its canonical owner. Owner-unresolved contracts fail all eleven projection items to the unsupported copy and withhold the detailed list. The structure is immutable review projection data, not evidence storage or a schema proposal.

It shows no uploaded file, real note, person, organization, source record, timestamped participant action, signature, customer, case, credit fact, or realistic identifier.

Purpose-bound evidence cannot be reused across pathways by implication. Conflicting sources produce **Needs human review**.

## 14. Correction and appeal experience

The interface may offer only fixed educational controls labeled:

- **Preview correction path**; and
- **Preview appeal state**.

It must not say submit, send, file, open case, assign, schedule, or contact reviewer.

A correction explains how a source fact could be challenged with its owner. An appeal explains how a review decision could be challenged by an authorized human. The hypothetical appeal reviewer is distinct from the original reviewer where policy requires independence. Growth and Kai never decide either path.

Every example states that it is fictional and no record exists.

## 15. Deterministic rejection patterns

The reviewer can inspect fixed examples for:

- self-review or reviewer conflict;
- collusive or reciprocal review;
- duplicate or replayed evidence;
- completion farming or popularity substitution;
- plagiarism or fabricated source provenance;
- retaliatory review;
- PII in a contribution;
- unauthorized visibility;
- expired, superseded, or conflicting evidence; and
- using a source outside its stated purpose.

The page must describe these as rejection/control examples, not as detected fraud or an accusation about a person.

## 16. Kai Growth Advisor

Kai remains **Credit Intelligence Officer**. Growth Advisor is a route-local review mode.

- What does this pathway help an operator practice?
- Which canonical owner would hold the future source fact?

- What happens when sources conflict?
