## Executive decision

CreditVector should approve a **two-loop economic model** while authorizing **no live economics**:

1. **Agency Builder loop — prove first, fund later.** Run a fixed invited shadow cohort called **Agency Ecosystem Stewardship Qualification**. It uses no money, points, ranks, percentages, or estimated earnings. Active status only makes an actor’s own evidence admissible. Organization integrity and health are gates; one accepted organization-level contribution category is the first shadow input. If the shadow run cannot prove organization-level value distinct from the direct work of individual operators, Agency Growth Distribution should not launch.
2. **Professional Operator loop — buyer-funded direct value first.** The first future money-bearing research candidate should be narrowly defined **B2B operator professional-development mentoring**, followed by instructor-led B2B operator education/training/speaking, where a business buyer accepts delivery and the lawful payee is determined by the actual contract and worker relationship. The approved curriculum must exclude consumer-specific credit improvement, dispute execution, legal advice, bureau/creditor representation, advance-fee guidance, and outcome claims. Courses, templates, marketplace infrastructure, consulting, and Kai Assets follow only after repeat demand, specialist review, and fully loaded unit economics are proven. Growth Partner/affiliate economics come last, if ever.

This sequence honors the Founder’s thesis without paying for generalized “success” before CreditVector can identify the consideration, funding source, causal value, and operating cost.

## 2. Dream state and operating thesis

The Growth Network succeeds when a participant can point to an explainable chain:

```text
real buyer or ecosystem need
  → independently owned source evidence
  → accepted education, mentorship, creation, or stewardship
  → stronger operator, customer, and organization outcomes
  → durable demand or verified incremental GCL value
  → transparent, bounded economic treatment
```

It fails when compensation can rise because more people joined, paid for participation/access/qualification or hierarchy position, stayed trapped, posted more content, raised a score, or sat below someone else in a hierarchy. A separately approved direct buyer payment may produce only the lawful provider’s own bona fide accepted-delivery proceeds under the Path B rule.

The decisive economic test is not gross transaction value, subscription count, distribution volume, or claimed engagement. Each stream needs a defined accounting base that counts every revenue and cost once:

```text
Agency net incremental value
  = verified incremental nonparticipant end-customer revenue versus matched baseline,
    or other counsel-approved participant-independent company value
  − the customer-level variable costs included in that revenue base
  − Agency distributions and Agency-only review, appeal, integrity, support,
    tax, accounting, payment, privacy, security, legal, reserve,
    discovery/CAC, and variable infrastructure/AI costs not already deducted

Professional stream contribution
  = platform fee revenue
  − refunds, chargebacks, provider cost, variable infrastructure/AI,
    discovery/CAC, moderation, support, integrity, tax, accounting,
    privacy, security, legal, and reserve costs not already deducted

Portfolio contribution
  = sum of stream contributions
  − shared incremental costs not allocated to or deducted in any stream,
    counted once
  with inter-stream transfers eliminated
```

Every term must state whether it is gross or net and which deductions it already includes. If fully loaded incremental value cannot be established without double counting, the relevant program remains recognition-only or is stopped.

**Design intent, not legal characterization:** a prospective, capped, company-funded stewardship program. #### Phase 0.5 shadow

- Fixed, invited design-partner cohort.
- One commonly controlled business group counts as one cohort participant.
- No currency, points, estimated amounts, ranks, tiers, or leaderboards.
- One accepted organization-level **ecosystem stewardship** evidence category.
- Integrity/remediation and atomic organization-health facts are eligibility floors, never positive multipliers.
- Operator count, operator addition, subscription, purchase, paid retention, credit outcomes, customer dispute volume, and popularity are prohibited inputs.
- The cohort and evidence policy freeze before the observation window.
- Every result is labeled **SHADOW · NON-MONETARY · CREATES NO ENTITLEMENT**.

Two modes must remain distinct:

- **Qualification shadow:** no monetary values or payable units; may become participant-visible only after separate privacy, UX, and compliance approval.
- **Internal financial simulation:** may use synthetic or access-restricted hypothetical amounts for Finance stress testing; never participant-visible, never a statement/balance, and never promotable into an obligation.

#### First possible live Agency model — only after all gates

Use **equal participation units from a prospectively budgeted, company-funded, maximum-liability-capped monthly pool**, not fixed percentages, tiers, or per-head amounts. “Company-funded” states the approved source and maximum liability; it does not imply escrow, segregation, full cash backing, or statutory safeguarding. Decision-specific backing remains TBD by Accounting/Finance/Counsel.

```text
approved cycle maximum liability
  = lesser of:
      A. Founder/Finance fixed cycle budget; and
      B. Finance-verified affordability ceiling from an approved
         participant-independent company source after declared costs

participant provisional share
  = one equal unit among qualified, commonly-controlled business groups

participant concentration cap
  = TBD by Founder and Finance from the approved cohort and risk model

undistributed or capped remainder
  = remains undistributed GCL company funds; never participant property and
    never reallocated in-cycle to intensify competition
```

The actual dollar budget and concentration cap must be set from verified financials by Founder and Finance before any economic implementation. A **10% common-control concentration cap** may be stress-tested in internal financial simulation, but it is not ratified here and is not a legal, anti-pyramid, accounting, or fairness safe harbor.

Only verified incremental margin from nonparticipant end-customer transactions—or another counsel-approved participant-independent company funding source—may be studied as a company-level affordability ceiling after all customer-level variable costs. Payments by any CGn participant, applicant, economically eligible person, or commonly controlled entity are excluded. Participant purchases and participant retention, individually and in aggregate, may never affect whether a pool opens, the pool size, qualification, or allocation. Counsel must define “nonparticipant end-customer” and conversion/common-control treatment before use.

#### Mature model — conditional, not a launch promise

Only after at least **three qualification-shadow cycles** and a controlled live pilot prove causality, cost, explainability, and no recruiting correlation, consider a **base-plus-capped-quality-unit hybrid**:

- one base unit for a qualified organization;
- no more than three quality categories;
- each category independently capped;
- total quality units for one qualified organization cannot exceed that organization’s base unit;
- no continuous scalar score;
- no tiers, ranks, titles, multipliers, thresholds, or cliff promotions;
- every category must represent organization-level consideration distinct from an operator’s directly paid service.

If that distinct consideration cannot be shown, retire Agency Growth Distribution and pay Professional Operators only for their own accepted work.

## 4. Professional Operator decision

### 4.1 Path architecture

A Professional Operator participates only through their own accepted product, service, licensed asset, or contract. Membership or organization ownership alone does not transfer an economic claim. The lawful payee and economic beneficiary must be determined per stream from the actual contracting party, worker relationship, assignment rules, informed consent, tax treatment, and applicable law. “Marketplace” is a distribution channel, not a separate type of contribution, and the same underlying transaction cannot be counted as multiple revenue streams.

A CGn participant may be the voluntary buyer of another participant’s bona fide direct product or service only under a separately approved contract and deterministic related-party policy. The lawful provider may receive proceeds only for its own accepted delivery. The purchase cannot improve either party’s eligibility, Agency pool, allocation, Growth Reputation, rank, seller access, or fee rate, and it cannot create an override or residual.

“Launch” below means **first eligible for a separately authorized controlled future pilot**, not current activation.

| Income path | Sequence | Recommendation |

### 5.2 Funding, reserve taxonomy, holds, and caps

- **Company funding allocation:** fixed before a live cycle opens and sourced from verified nonparticipant end-customer margin or another counsel-approved participant-independent company source. Payments by a CGn participant, applicant, economically eligible person, or commonly controlled entity are excluded individually and in aggregate. Never funded from participant purchases, required subscriptions, later participants, or another participant’s withheld amount.
- **Approved-obligation backing:** accountant-defined backing policy for approved unpaid obligations; its amount and instrument are TBD. A 100% cash scenario may be stress-tested internally, but it is not proof of company solvency and does not replace treatment of pending liabilities, taxes, refunds, provider reserves, or statutory safeguarding.
- **Accounting allowance:** accountant-owned estimate for expected loss/refund/error treatment; not participant property and not a product hold.
- **Provider reserve:** provider-specific settlement control, separately disclosed and reconciled; not interchangeable with a GCL program allocation.
- **Statutory safeguarding assets:** only if payments counsel identifies a legal duty; separately owned and governed.
- **Program contingency:** amount and risk horizon are **TBD BY FINANCE/ACCOUNTING FROM VERIFIED MODEL**.
- **Concentration:** a prospective common-control cap is mandatory; the final value is **TBD BY FOUNDER/FINANCE**. A 10% cap is internal simulation only, not a safe harbor.
- **Blanket participant holdback:** do not use one for approved Agency Distribution obligations. Mature evidence before approval instead.
- **Risk-specific hold:** allowed only before settlement for a documented risk, with reason code, scope, owner, evidence, review date, and appeal. No indefinite hold.
- **Direct-sale reserve:** mentorship, education, marketplace, and other sale proceeds require their own refund/chargeback reserve policy; they cannot borrow the Agency policy.
- **No cross-subsidy:** one stream’s participant proceeds do not fund another stream’s obligations or losses.

### 5.4 Tax posture

- Determine the legal payor, payee, recipient type, jurisdiction, and income characterization per stream before any payable status.
- Collect validated tax documentation only when a live relationship is separately authorized; Phase 0.5 collects none.
- Store tax identifiers in a segregated encrypted vault with purpose-limited access; Kai, Growth Reputation, analytics, and fraud models receive no raw TIN.
- Do not hard-code an information-reporting threshold. Current 2026 authority must be selected by the tax specialist from the actual rail and relationship; thresholds are reporting rules, not tax-free allowances. See [IRS Publication 15 (2026)](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15), [IRS Internal Revenue Bulletin 2026-19](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2026-19_IRB), [Form W-9 requester instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw9), [Form 1099-K FAQ](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/form-1099-k-faqs-general-information), and [IRS backup withholding guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/backup-withholding).
- Withhold only when legally required or legally permitted under the approved payer, payee, withholding, and jurisdiction analysis. Never describe tax withholding as tax advice or promise a recipient’s classification.
- A dollar payout threshold does not remove tax reporting, recordkeeping, or unclaimed-property duties.

## 9. Security, integrity, and anti-fraud architecture

### 9.1 Threat model

| Threat | Prevent/detect design |
|---|---|
| Self-referral / fake referral | Prohibit participant recruitment economics; later external Growth Partner requires beneficial-owner, device/session, attribution, refund, and related-party checks |
| Duplicate/Sybil accounts | Verified Identity, common-control graph, uniqueness checks, no per-account benefit, and manual review |
| Shell organizations | Aggregate beneficial/common control; one participation unit per controlled group; require legitimate operating history |
| Inactive-seat inflation | Operator count is never economic; active status only validates the actor’s own evidence |
| Retention manipulation | No positive retained-headcount weight; fair-exit and complaint signals are downside/integrity facts |
| Reciprocal/collusive mentoring | Related-party disclosure, reciprocal-pattern detection, accepted delivery, beneficiary sampling, caps, and separation of reviewer |
| Contribution flooding | No raw-volume, views, minutes, or completion pay; source acceptance, category caps, deduplication, plagiarism checks |
| Circular sales/refunds | Buyer/seller/common-control checks, settlement maturity, refund linkage, velocity controls, and no duplicate economic use |
| Attribution hijacking | Short disclosed attribution rules, last-valid eligible external customer event, signed event evidence, self-referral rejection, and dispute path |
| Reviewer abuse | Maker-checker, conflict declaration, least privilege, immutable reason history, override reporting, independent appeal |
| Kai manipulation | Read-only allowlisted tools, prompt-injection boundaries, untrusted-content fencing, no secrets, structured outputs, human confirmation |
| Insider/provider fraud | Segregation of duties, dual authorization, reconciliation, immutable journal, provider receipt, no shared credentials |

### 9.2 Fail-closed laws

- Unknown identity, ownership, source, policy, version, timezone, currency, payee, tax status, or evidence state means **not eligible/not payable**, never best-effort inference.
- An anomaly signal can open or inform a case; it cannot silently punish.
- Fraud labels are private, reason-coded, evidence-backed, access-controlled, appealable, and retained only as required.
- AI may summarize signals, never render the final adverse economic decision.
- Economic cases and participant support must not expose detection thresholds that materially enable evasion.

## 11. Tradeoffs and devil’s-advocate review

### Recommended model advantages

- directly tests the non-recruiting organization thesis without creating participant expectations;
- first future revenue comes from a buyer receiving a defined service, making consideration easier to explain;
- fixed pools bound Agency liability and prevent a percentage from silently outrunning margin;
- equal pilot units avoid false precision and score tuning;
- Reputation, XP, Arena, Community, Billing, and source domains remain distinct;
- membership alone cannot transfer an economic claim; the lawful payee follows the actual contract, worker relationship, consent, tax treatment, and law;
- policy freeze, common-control aggregation, source provenance, and appeal reduce gaming and arbitrary treatment.

### Costs and limitations

- Agency Distribution may never prove distinct enough to justify money;
- fixed pools dilute as qualified cohorts grow and can feel like a pay cut;
- a 15th cadence creates salary/residual expectations even with careful copy;
- evidence review, disputes, moderation, and tax administration may cost more than small earnings;
- manual pilots do not prove scale and may select unusually motivated participants;
- live mentoring and education create refunds, no-shows, content, worker-classification, and credit-services risk;
- common-control and collusion detection can produce privacy and false-positive risk;
- strict no-double-use rules reduce apparent program generosity but preserve economic integrity;
- deferring Marketplace and affiliate infrastructure slows breadth but avoids an expensive empty shelf and the highest-fraud channel.

### Kill criteria

Stop or remain recognition-only if any of the following occurs:

- Agency qualification or modeled allocation correlates materially with operator additions, headcount, purchases, paid retention, or blocked exits;
- organization-level consideration cannot be separated from an operator’s directly paid work;
- fully loaded incremental contribution margin is not positive at the Founder-approved horizon;
- complaint, appeal, reversal, refund, concentration, false-positive, or manual-review burden exceeds the predeclared threshold;
- participants misunderstand the shadow as promised income despite tested disclosures;
- protected-class, small-organization, new-organization, or accessibility analysis shows unjustified disparate impact;
- evidence provenance or deterministic replay cannot be demonstrated;
- counsel, accounting, tax, privacy, security, worker, or payments analysis produces an unresolved red gate.

The Founder and Finance must set numeric success/kill thresholds from baseline data before a shadow run. Engineering must not invent them.
