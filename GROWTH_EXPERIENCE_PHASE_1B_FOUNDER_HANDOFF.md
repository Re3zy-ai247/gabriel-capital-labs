## Executive summary

The Phase 1B capability contract turns the approved Growth Center direction into a precise non-monetary operating vocabulary. It shows how an operator could build capability through professional development, mentorship preparation, education, useful creation, Community contribution, and organizational stewardship while preserving the canonical owners of every future source fact.

### 2. One-owner law

Growth composes authorized concepts; it does not become Identity, Organizations, Membership, Performance Intelligence, Learning, Community, Operator Network, Marketplace, Reputation, Agency Command, Meetings, Documents, or Kai. An unresolved owner is unavailable rather than guessed.

### 3. Eight independent state lanes

The contract separates availability/contract support, preparation/participation, evidence sufficiency, completion, source review, correction, appeal, and visibility. No state becomes an opaque progress score or a proxy for qualification.

### 4. Exact selectable fixtures

The implementation exposes exactly: `overview`, `empty`, `preparing`, `completed-unreviewed`, `in-review`, `changes-requested`, `source-corrected`, `appeal-in-review`, `privacy-restricted`, and `unsupported`.

Mentorship refuses `completed-unreviewed` and `appeal-in-review`; organizational stewardship refuses `appeal-in-review`; operator-created contribution remains owner-unresolved. Unknown, duplicate, partial, malformed, and invalid contract/fixture requests fail closed. Not-accepted, source-conflict, source-owner-review, and resolved-appeal terms remain conceptual future vocabulary unless an exact fixture represents them. The rejection panel is not a fixture or detector.

### 5. Evidence and review

The Preview shows one immutable eleven-part structured evidence preview: professional purpose, source owner, subject/scope, evidence category, occurrence/source confirmation, reviewer role, policy/contract version, visibility, correction route, appeal route, and expiry/supersession. ### 6. Privacy

No participant, customer, organization, case, file, PII, protected attribute, employment evaluation, or consumer credit fact is read, collected, inferred, stored, or transmitted. Public and cross-organization visibility are unsupported.

### 7. Kai

Kai remains Credit Intelligence Officer. Growth Advisor is a route-local deterministic review mode. Kai explains a selected fixed fixture, names its canonical owner and boundary, and takes no action.

### 8. Unsupported behavior

Unknown, multiple, malformed, impossible, conflicting, or unauthorized selections render:
