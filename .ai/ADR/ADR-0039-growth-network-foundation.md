# ADR-0039: CreditVector Growth Network dormant foundation

Status: **PROPOSED — DORMANT FOUNDATION; ECONOMIC DECISION UNRATIFIED**
Date: 2026-07-31
Decision owner: Founder

## Context

CreditVector needs bounded vocabulary for possible Agency Builder and Professional Operator growth paths without creating a participant program, source record, economic right, or production surface.

## Decision

Maintain a schema-free, effect-free Growth Network foundation that may define synthetic vocabulary and future contract seams only. It may not own or reinterpret Identity, Organizations, Membership, Reputation, Performance Intelligence, Community, Marketplace, Billing, tax, provider, or payout truth.

The Founder Economic Decision Matrix remains the sole future economic ratification record. It is unratified. The foundation therefore creates no participant eligibility, distribution, fee, pool, allocation, payment, earnings claim, or money movement.

## Invariants

- Contribution may be studied; recruiting and popularity are not rewarded.
- Growth Reputation is not XP, cash, a public rating, or a canonical source record.
- Future money, tax, worker-classification, payments, and participant terms require separate specialist and Founder gates.
- No new ownership is established through this ADR. The frozen ownership registry controls.

## Consequences

The foundation may be reused by protected synthetic Founder previews. It is not a live bounded context and authorizes no schema, API, event, runtime model, data integration, commerce, public access, merge, or production deployment.

**CGN ECONOMIC PHASE 1A — BLOCKED.**
