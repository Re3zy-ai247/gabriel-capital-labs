# Media OS — recurring content series & cadence (the marketing operating system)

Marketing stops being campaigns and becomes **programming**: fixed series, fixed formats, fixed pipelines — every episode is a fill-in-the-blanks production, not a reinvention. Concepts pool: `MARKETING-CONCEPTS.md` (MK-*) — series below are the recurring engine; MK one-offs slot between them. Production mechanics: `CONTENT-PRODUCTION-PIPELINE.md`. All series obey `REY-KAI-DYNAMIC.md` + `CINEMATIC-BIBLE.md` + the CROA bar.

## Repeatable episode pipeline (deterministic)
1. **Slot** — pick series + episode topic from the source-material queue (Brief items, community questions, myth list). No topic invention from scratch.
2. **Script** — fill the series template with the topic; Rey/Kai lines follow REY-KAI-DYNAMIC patterns verbatim.
3. **Gate** — `/compliance-review` on script (templates pre-cleared; per-episode review is fast because only the slots changed).
4. **Assemble** — registered assets first (Kai states, panel components, UI captures, music bed); new renders only through the Creative-OS pipeline.
5. **Cut** — CINEMATIC-BIBLE rhythm; 16:9 master → 9:16 crop.
6. **Founder approve** (🟡 publish gate) → post → log in `../marketing/CAMPAIGN-LIBRARY.md` (series ID + episode #).
7. **Measure** — per-series metrics into `../business-intelligence/METRICS.md` BI-MKT rows once channels are instrumented; kill or double series quarterly on evidence.
