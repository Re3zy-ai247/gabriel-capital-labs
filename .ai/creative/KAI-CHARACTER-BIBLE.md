- **Breed/build:** red Shiba Inu, adult, compact athletic frame; alert upright posture; slight forward-friendly head carriage.
- **Coat:** warm red-orange (sesame-red) dorsal coat; classic **urajiro** — cream/white on muzzle sides, cheeks, brow spots, chest bib, belly, inner legs; **white socks** on all four paws (front socks higher); NO black overlay saddle.
- **Head:** broad rounded skull; refined tapering muzzle with white blaze up the center; **black nose**; whisker set clearly visible; subtle "smile" at the lip line at rest.
- **Eyes:** dark brown-black, almond, calm and direct; slight soft catchlight; NOT enlarged, NOT sparkly-cartoon.
- **Ears:** small triangular, thick, slightly rounded tips, canted a touch forward; cream inner-ear fur.
- **Tail:** classic sickle curl over the back, cream underside.
- **Expression at rest:** attentive calm bordering on a knowing smile — the second and fourth reference photos are the personality target.
