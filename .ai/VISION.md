# CreditVector Vision — the next 5 years

> Direction, not a roadmap (sequencing lives in `ROADMAP.md` + the AIOS backlog). Status: PROPOSED by the founder's Phase-2 mandate (2026-07-12); every step below still passes the Constitution, the five-review gate, and counsel where required. Nothing here overrides the CROA bar.

## What CreditVector is becoming
**The intelligence operating system for consumer finance and consumer rights.** Not a credit-repair tool — the platform a consumer, educator, or agency runs their entire financial-rights life on. Reference points: Salesforce (system of record), Notion (knowledge), Stripe (infrastructure), Linear (operating discipline), Palantir (intelligence over data) — composed into one coherent AI platform.

## The five horizons
1. **Dispute intelligence (LIVE today).** The best-grounded, most compliance-safe dispute engine in the market: per-bureau truth, honest scoring, deterministic letters, response intelligence. This is the wedge — it stays the quality bar.
2. **Consumer finance intelligence.** From "fix my report" to "understand and run my financial life": score forecasting, credit-builder guidance, spending/utilization intelligence, life-event playbooks (mortgage-ready, auto, rental) — always educational, never advisory beyond the compliance bar.
3. **Consumer-rights & legal education.** FCRA/FDCPA/CROA knowledge as a product: plain-English statute intelligence, CFPB/FTC signal tracking (the Brief is the seed), interactive rights education. AI legal *education*, never legal advice.
4. **Agency & professional infrastructure.** Agency OS as the Stripe-of-credit-services: client workspaces (live), then bulk operations, white-label surfaces, audit trails, agency compliance tooling — the infrastructure layer legitimate agencies standardize on.
5. **Business credit, funding & ecosystem.** The "coming soon" modules already in `lib/brand.ts` (Credit Builder, Funding Hub, Business Credit) mature into a marketplace/ecosystem: vetted partners, data integrations, an API surface — CreditVector as platform, not app.

## What compounds
- **The knowledge graph** (`knowledge/GRAPH.md`): every feature, ruling, campaign, and support pattern becomes queryable intelligence — eventually consumed by Kai.
- **Kai** grows from community answerer → master agent across the whole platform (each capability expansion = ADR + security review; ADR-0005 constraints hold until deliberately superseded).
- **Trust**: the compliance-first posture is the moat. Competitors sell myths; CreditVector sells truth with receipts.
