Scope decision

The feature matrix supports a consumer-only, self-mail, invite-only beta after the P0 gates close. External Agency onboarding, paid live acquisition, Community, Arena, Growth, Marketplace, public profiles, physical mail, and autonomous Kai actions remain excluded unless separately reviewed and gated.

Verdict

NO-GO. Repository strength does not substitute for production, provider, counsel, restore, alert, and authenticated browser evidence.
