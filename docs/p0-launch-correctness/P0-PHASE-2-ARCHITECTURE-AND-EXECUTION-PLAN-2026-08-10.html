6. Trust boundaries

Every operation uses a server-owned principal: actorId + tenantId + consumerId + authorizationKind + authorizationVersion. Agency grants are revalidated. IDs, cookies, model output, tracking refs, and provider refs are selectors—not authority.

  • Authorize before lookup/decrypt.
  • No bare-ID repository operations.
  • AAD binds scope, entity/version, purpose, and key/schema version.
  • Adapters cannot choose tenants or hold general DB credentials.
  • Callbacks are signature/timestamp/replay checked before parsing and exact correlation.
  • Every write is read back and semantically verified.

7. Round 0 architecture

Round 0 reviews legal name, aliases, current/former addresses, safe identity fields, phones and employment actually reported, mixed-file indicators, and unrecognized accounts. The consumer sees bureau, provenance, uncertainty, and a safely masked value.

REPORTED, CONFIRMED, DISPUTED, UNKNOWN, NOT APPLICABLE are product projections. The immutable baseline's IdentityFact.classification is the sole per-fact disposition; NOT APPLICABLE is category completion only when no fact exists. Accurate former addresses and legitimate employment can be confirmed. Merely unfamiliar information is not automatically false or disputable. A machine mixed-file flag is not testimony.

A confirmed baseline is a new immutable version; later reports supersede rather than rewrite it.

8. Consumer confirmation architecture

Account facts retain the existing exact ConsumerAssertion. Identity facts retain canonical IdentityFact.classification; a separate exact correspondence claim receipt pins that classification and purpose without carrying another disposition. No nullable account FK or fake account.

observation -> neutral consumer review -> bounded disposition
-> server re-read of exact source -> append account assertion or identity claim receipt
-> readback/attestation
-> downstream receives only exact assertion bindings

No leading default, AI testimony, silent confirmation, stale replay, or cross-bureau/field/report reuse. New evidence revision or digest requires reconfirmation.

tenant + consumer + case + recipient/type/address

Every item also pins assertion, disputed field, observation, assessment, creditor/furnisher, masked account reference, requested action, evidence provenance, one exact CONSUMER_SELECTED action decision, and its eligible 2B policy-evaluation receipt. Requested action is a closed code, not free-form claim authority. Restricted items consolidate only when normalized restriction digests match.

  • Unknown compatibility or counsel state is NOT_READY.

10. Canonical artifact architecture

Sender identity is exact membership, never a current-profile lookup: selected legal-name and return-address fact IDs/roles, baseline/version, classification/integrity/order, and senderManifestSha256 are pinned through Packet, Artifact, approval, Mailing, and fulfillment.

Preview = download = print = future fulfillment bytes. No regeneration. The mailed letter excludes browser URL/title/timestamp, CreditVector educational footer, and UI instructions. A separate DO NOT MAIL THIS PAGE guide is excluded from fulfillment.

11. Mail Center / Evidence Ledger

Mail Center becomes a projection of Packet, Artifact, Mailing, attempts, and evidence—not a package generator. MailingEvent is the sole semantic transition chain; current state is derived or a rebuildable CAS cache. EvidenceEvent is a same-transaction refs-only audit/index/outbox, never another state authority. Sensitive access is separately audited.

Self-mail requires approval of the exact artifact/packet/sender/recipient tuple and explicit bounded sent evidence. Future fulfillment reattests that tuple, consumes an exact artifact capability and pinned address, then verifies create/retrieve/callback evidence. The core is vendor-neutral. After self-mail sent attestation or provider submission begins, content, membership, sender, recipient address, and bytes cannot change; corrections create a new version/mailing.

12. Response Intelligence

  1. Authorize scope.
  2. Store/read back immutable response bytes.
  3. Associate to exact mailing/recipient/packet/version/report/round.
  4. Link findings to exact items/accounts/bureaus.
  5. Quarantine ambiguity.
  6. Persist provisional, uncertainty-preserving interpretation.

Filename, OCR text, tracking number, recipient name, and model guess cannot authorize association. Kai can summarize evidence but cannot manufacture a legal conclusion.

14. Progress Intelligence

Report-derived scores are primary; manual scores are secondary and provenance-labeled; missing scores remain missing. Views cover bureau/model-compatible trajectories, changed/new/no-longer-reported accounts, corrections, unresolved issues, balance/status/remarks movement, correspondence, delivery, responses, uncertainty, and not-comparable periods.

Allowed: “Experian score increased 18 points between comparable reports.” Prohibited: “Deleting Account X caused the increase.”

15. Kai boundary

Kai receives a server-built evidence envelope with provenance, completeness, uncertainty, assertion state, policy status, and allowed review verbs. It may explain, compare, teach, and guide review. It may not confirm facts, create assertions/outcomes, select actions/recipients, approve/send, override counsel, or claim causality. All proposed actions are revalidated server-side.
