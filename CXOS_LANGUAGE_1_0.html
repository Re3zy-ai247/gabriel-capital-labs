02 · Facility grammar

One institution, distinct rooms

The rooms share a ground, palette, typographic discipline, motion vocabulary, and truth bar. They differ through purpose, light direction, geometry, density, and the kind of authority they express.

Shared facility laws

  • Ink ground; navy, ocean, brand teal, status colors, and verified-success green.
  • Plus Jakarta Sans in production; tabular numerals for operational values.
  • One light source per room and three visual planes at most.
  • Semantic content remains complete in motion, static, constrained, and reduced projections.

One protagonist

  • Mission Control: command threshold.
  • Passage: destination aperture.
  • Arena arrival: recognition register.
  • Agency Command: Kai brief and its single ranked action.

Architecture before containers

Build with axes, banks, rails, ledgers, thresholds, planes, and stations. Cards are allowed only when the card itself is the interaction.

Room purpose

Mission Control observes. The Passage transfers. The Arena recognizes. Agency Command coordinates. Founder instrumentation inspects without becoming part of the room.

Spatial vocabulary

Room
A complete operating environment with one purpose, protagonist, and settled truth.
Chamber
The bounded volume that gives a room its authority and light logic.
Floor
The native-scroll document plane through which the operator inspects stations.
Threshold
An explicit boundary where authority, state, or environment changes.
Station
One semantic stop responsible for one class of evidence or operation.
Console
A contained interactive instrument with a truthful state, control, and destination.
Horizon
A stable depth or capacity reference that orients without becoming a fake live chart.
Axis
The directional line connecting the protagonist, supporting instruments, and threshold.

Meaningful asymmetry replaces equal-card grids. Content is embedded in shared planes, ledgers, banks, stations, and consoles. Meaningful negative space clarifies authority, travel, and hierarchy. The operational center of gravity remains visible at every viewport.

Information architecture

Primary operational truth
The dominant verified fact answering what is happening now.
Secondary instrumentation
Supporting measures that explain without competing.
Evidence
The source, receipt, ledger entry, or deterministic input behind a claim.
Status
The current state expressed in text and structure, never color alone.
Action
One explicit, permission-valid control with a truthful destination and outcome.
Observation
A bounded interpretation visually distinct from canonical fact.
Explanation
Why a state exists, using disclosed drivers rather than invented causality.
Escalation
An issue needing authority or attention, with reason, destination, and safe next step.

Primary truth and action define the center of gravity. Evidence and status substantiate it. Secondary instrumentation, observation, explanation, and escalation remain subordinate and may not manufacture urgency.

Five governing experience laws

Approved direction · proposed Phase 6.2 application. These five laws govern Agency Command’s Phase 6.2 language release and every later operating-room proposal. They do not claim that Mission Control or any future room has already been modified.

Observable heartbeat

Every operating room requires an observable heartbeat. Motion, rhythm, and ambient state must express that room’s actual purpose without changing canonical facts or fabricating live activity.

Deliberate arrival and exit

Every major room requires a deliberate arrival and exit. Arrival establishes identity, scope, source readiness, and settled truth; exit preserves orientation, names the destination, returns control, and never disguises navigation, error, or mutation. Tier C/D, reduced-motion, and static projections carry the same truth without required motion.

Spatial internal operating districts

Every major room must express its internal operating districts spatially. Their position expresses purpose, ownership, sequence, evidence, and authority. Banks, rails, ledgers, stations, horizons, consoles, and thresholds replace disconnected cards; responsive order preserves the relationships.

Continuous contextual Kai, not disconnected tools

Kai must appear as a continuous contextual executive channel, not a disconnected list of tools. It connects the current district’s source, interpretation, recommendation, and safe next action rather than appearing as disconnected assistants, workflow buttons, or chat widgets. Continuity never invents memory, context, or authority.

Deterministic, authorized, truthfully sourced personalization

Every personalized fact, interpretation, recommendation, draft, or boundary names its source class, stays within the current user, tenant, role, capability, and consent boundary, and never exceeds the evidence. Source domains own facts; Kai interprets and prepares; the operator reviews and decides; execution requires separate explicit authorization.

Phase 6.2 Agency Command application: deterministic heartbeat; bounded arrival and Mission Control return; Executive Brief, heartbeat, queue, portfolio, health/capacity, and command districts; one contextual command surface mapped to eleven fixed fixture capabilities; and deterministic supported/unsupported, revise/cancel, and no-action outcomes. Command state is route-instance-only and clears on refresh or exit.

Future governance: Mission Control and future rooms must separately document their five-law mapping, canonical sources, ownership, motion budget, static equivalent, authorization, and failure behavior before implementation. Phase 6.2 changes no Mission Control or future-room runtime, data source, interaction, or release status.

Boundary: the Phase 6.2 command surface uses fictional fixtures only—no live AI or model, customer data, API call, storage destination, calendar or task system, contact action, customer mutation, or fabricated completion.

04 · Passage grammar

Departure must be earned

The Passage is not an interstitial. It is a deterministic transfer from a visible origin to a semantic destination.

Origin and power-down

The origin stays legible while instruments recede and the axis contracts. Early input cancels to the visible origin; later input settles forward. An unexplained black screen is forbidden.

Hallway and anticipation

Filled cool planes converge toward a distant aperture. Warmth arrives late. No HUD, marketing slogan, or data card competes with the corridor.

Conversion and threshold

Rectilinear blue becomes radial ceremonial architecture. The final Passage frame and settled Arena frame share focal position, scale, geometry, and light.

Settlement and return

Focus moves to the visible destination before the overlay unmounts. Static projection scrolls to real content. Return restores focus, scroll, inert state, timers, and one settled origin.

Complete transition language

  1. Acknowledgment: confirm the initiating action without implying an unperformed write or outcome.
  2. Departure: recede the origin while preserving orientation.
  3. Travel: establish legible depth and movement through one facility.
  4. Anticipation: withhold destination authority until the intended beat.
  5. Conversion: transform origin geometry into destination grammar.
  6. Threshold: make the boundary and destination undeniable.
  7. Arrival: settle into the same semantic destination already beneath the cinematic layer.
  8. Operation: return control to the native document with no residual lock, timer, or cinematic state.
  9. Return: restore focus, scroll, inert state, timers, and the origin’s settled truth deterministically.
05 · Arena grammar

Recognition, not gamification

The Arena is one inhabited ceremonial room whose authority comes from evidence, not scarcity or spectacle.

  1. Station A reserves a full-viewport final composition before recognition appears.
  2. Clearance, standing, evidence, lifetime record, and Kai truth remain in normal document flow.
  3. Evidence is an engraved ledger; milestones are earned seals.
  4. Native scroll walks stations II–V. No scroll lock or mobile pinned stage.
  5. Viewport-bound atmosphere unifies the chamber without reducing readability.
  6. Competition remains planned until real policy and data exist.
  7. Reduced motion receives the same complete ceremony at rest.

Agency Health

Use HEALTHY, WATCH, or AT RISK with response attention, oldest queue age, capacity, and source coverage. A 0–100 value remains a labeled specimen until its formula is ratified.

Kai brief

Two short sentences, one ranked action, one receipt. No chatbot, avatar, mascot, or recommendation stack. One bounded contextual command surface maps to route-instance note, reminder, scheduling, summary, task-preparation, bottleneck, follow-up, organization, meeting, explanation, and suggested-action previews. Every output remains deterministic, truthfully sourced, non-persistent, operator-reviewed, and ends with a no-action receipt.

Portfolio work

Use an ordered priority ledger and border-row client ledger. Inline inspection is local and reversible; a review route never opens a real customer workspace.

Team/load truth

Solo projection shows unavailable. Only an explicit Team Specimen may show synthetic seats and load, under a second permanent disclosure and with no fake management action.

Minimum arrival threshold

Tier A: at most 1.5 seconds for review identity → fixture systems → capacity horizon → ledger activation → Kai focus → settled heartbeat. Tier B: at most 700 ms on one plane. Mission Control return: 360–480 ms acknowledgment and departure, then ordinary review-route navigation. Tier C/D and reduced/static: zero. Content paints before motion; any input completes the settle in under 100 ms.

Living instruments

Client flow is a fixed-position rail; capacity is an occupied/reserve horizon; workload is disclosed queue composition; response aging is illustrative and not a legal deadline; evidence coverage preserves unlike source states; and bottlenecks name the waiting decision or evidence gate. Throughput stays “Not instrumented” until a time-bounded source model exists.

Kai workbench boundary

Fixture-only commands warn against real customer information. A local no-destination form may provide keyboard submission, but the form and field declare no name, action, method, form action, model, API, storage, calendar, notification, task, customer, assignment, or production destination. It never claims that anything was saved, sent, scheduled, assigned, created, contacted, or changed.

Phase 6.2 contextual command law

One bounded command is matched through a fixed deterministic intent table. Supported results name intent, classification, fixture sources, preview, and receipt. Unsupported, ambiguous, multi-intent, live-data, legal-outcome, and effect-seeking requests prepare nothing and never fall back silently.

Revision, cancellation, and ownership

Revision invalidates stale preview state and requires explicit re-preparation. Cancel and clear remove route-instance preview state only. User-entered notes remain OPERATOR DRAFT · USER-ENTERED · NOT VERIFIED BY KAI. Fixture domains own facts; the operator owns the command and decision; Kai owns neither customer records nor execution.

Static parity and compliance

Reduced-motion and static projections preserve identical command, source, classification, supported/unsupported, revise/cancel, and receipt information. Wording remains educational and process-bound: no guaranteed deletion, removal, score improvement, legal conclusion, absolute deadline, compliance certification, or credit-repair outcome.

07 · Visual language

Density signals authority

Specific operational hierarchy replaces generic polish. Data earns the pixels; decoration never substitutes for meaning.

Hierarchy and scale

Room identity is largest; primary operational truth follows; action, status, evidence, observation, explanation, and metadata descend in that order. Secondary instrumentation may not compete with the protagonist.

Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans in production. Display voice only for room identity. Tabular numerals for money, dates, scores, XP, counts, durations, and capacity. Mono only for instruments and receipts.

Color

Ink is ground. Ocean is process. Restrained cyan means the approved brand blue/teal operational role, not a new raw hue. Ceremonial gold means restrained existing amber and metal-like recognition tokens in the Arena, not Agency decoration. Success green is verified-positive only; amber/red are honest status.

Depth and light

Three planes maximum. Text never floats. Mission Control and Agency Command receive analytical light from above; Arena light rises from below.

Opacity, lines, and framing

Low opacity denotes atmosphere or subordinate instrumentation, never uncertain truth by itself. Lines express ownership, sequence, connection, or boundary. Rectilinear operational framing and radial recognition framing convert coherently at thresholds; atmospheric depth stays behind content.

Data

Prefer ledgers, queues, definition lists, and banks. Reject decorative donuts, animated counters, unlabelled sparklines, truncated deadlines, and color-only status.

08 · Motion language

Six primitives, one ease

Motion communicates state, system presence, spatial travel, recognition, and attention. It does not perform for its own sake.

State and ambient motion

State motion represents truthful power, focus, availability, or room-state change. Ambient motion represents bounded system presence and never implies changing data, team presence, or live activity.

Transition and recognition motion

Transition motion represents travel between an identified origin and destination. Recognition motion reveals existing evidence or status and never changes the canonical fact.

09 · Data-truth language

No atmosphere outranks truth

Synthetic review data must remain unmistakably synthetic to sighted visitors and assistive technology.

Synthetic disclosure

Visible: SYNTHETIC REVIEW · NO LIVE DATA. Complete assistive disclosure: no real account, database, customer record, reputation read, or reputation write.

Claims and receipts

Kai may interpret deterministic truth, but may not invent counts, deadlines, health, outcomes, or confidence. Every operational claim can name its source.

Personalization and ownership

Personalization is deterministic, permission-valid, tenant-scoped, consent-aware where applicable, and sourced from an owning domain. Synthetic review claims no real authorization or customer-data read. Kai does not own canonical facts, infer unavailable context, or perform execution.

State truth

Empty means no evidence. Unavailable means absent source or capability. Error states preserve what remains known. Permission states reveal no protected metadata.

Specimen truth

Numeric Agency Health, Team Load, pipeline, revenue, assignment, and automation remain visibly specimen-only until implemented and separately approved.

Evidence law: repository facts are authoritative and status must be evidence-backed. Fake live metrics, popularity ratings, decorative rankings, and invented operational outcomes are forbidden. Every claim must resolve to a source or deterministic receipt.

Interaction laws

  1. Actions name their destination and supported effect; dead controls and decorative action labels are forbidden.
  2. Controls truthfully expose availability, pressed, loading, disabled, unavailable, error, and success states.
  3. Non-modal surfaces make no false modal claim.
  4. No interaction performs a hidden state mutation, storage write, network write, or data mutation.
  5. Keyboard operation follows the same semantic order as touch and pointer operation.
  6. Focus continuity survives open, close, departure, arrival, projection change, and return.
  7. Return deterministically restores the focus target, native scroll, inert state, and settled room state.
  8. Native scroll remains authoritative; scroll hijacking and section snapping are forbidden.
  9. No user trap: cancel, skip, Escape, and safe return remain available wherever choreography could delay control.
  10. Natural-language command surfaces disclose deterministic matching, distinguish supported from unsupported intent, invalidate stale previews on revision, and report no action after prepare, cancel, or clear.
10 · Accessibility

The semantic room is the room

The cinematic layer decorates a complete document. It never carries information that disappears for keyboard, screen-reader, reduced-motion, or constrained users.

  • WCAG 2.2 AA target across every scene.
  • A visible skip-navigation link moves directly to the main landmark.
  • One main and one h1, with headings and landmarks matching visual hierarchy.
  • Cinematic world aria-hidden; semantic content remains in DOM order.
  • Live-region restraint: one polite status region for projection, journey, and fixture announcements, without duplication.
  • Visible focus; destination focus moves before overlay removal; focus never falls to body.
  • Director close returns focus to its pill; a non-modal instrument never claims modal semantics.
  • Touch/coarse controls are at least 44 px.
  • Pressed state and status use text/structure in addition to color.
  • Text and essential controls maintain readable WCAG contrast across atmospheric and interaction states.
  • Reduced motion retains information parity; the complete static alternative preserves every fact, action, disclosure, and destination.
  • Automated Axe evidence is necessary but does not replace manual assistive-technology review.

12 · Performance

Measure the actual room

Functional success does not erase a long task, and a clean screenshot does not prove stability.

Budgets

  • Marketing LCP target 1.8 s; budget 2.5 s at mobile P75.
  • INP below 200 ms.
  • Skip/settle response below 100 ms.
  • General CLS below 0.1; CXOS room-swap target 0.
  • Cinematic above-fold assets at most 300 kB.

Technology boundary

  • Semantic HTML and CSS are the complete foundation.
  • No new dependency for effects covered by CSS or platform APIs.
  • No autoplay video/audio, WebGL, Three.js, GSAP, Rive, or Lottie in core unless separately approved.
  • No video dependency, Canvas loop, continuous expensive JavaScript loop, or runtime asset spectacle.
  • The experience remains readable if every enhancement fails.

Long-task gate: motion is CSS/compositor-first and never animates layout. No sustained application-attributable long task is acceptable while ambient state is settled; repeated tasks above 50 ms during a transition block release.

13 · Founder review control

Visible, local, and non-mutating

Review instrumentation explains why an experience is static or cinematic and lets the Founder inspect states without changing browser, account, or production truth.

  1. Review controls exist only in review builds and are hard-off in production.
  2. One safe-area-aware Director pill opens a bounded, independently scrollable sheet.
  3. Escape closes the Director before journey Escape behavior and returns focus to the pill.
  4. Auto, Cinematic, and Static expose pressed state and the resolved tier/reason.
  5. Reduced-motion override requires explicit confirmation and changes no system setting.
  6. Data Saver, low-memory safety, and detection failure cannot be overridden.
  7. Projection choice and fixture state are separate fieldsets.
  8. Projection changes settle timers, pause, focus, scroll, inert state, and environment to one truth.
  9. Fixture changes are local and synthetic.
  10. The Director sends no data, stores no consent, and exposes no credential.

15 · Release gates

Experience approval is not integration approval

A room may be approved as a direction while production, architecture, data, and merge authority remain untouched.

Scope and public safety

  • Exact commit range and file allowlist recorded.
  • Specimen capability visibly labeled.
  • No secrets, local paths, usernames, tokens, environment values, or private evidence locations.
  • No unrelated agent infrastructure.

Static validation

  • Typecheck, touched-file lint, diff check, and CXOS guards pass.
  • Optimized review build passes without database mutation.
  • Manifests and lockfiles stay unchanged unless authorized.

Experience matrix

  • Desktop, tablet, mobile, coarse, and reduced/static.
  • Constrained fail-closed behavior.
  • Populated, empty, loading, unavailable, error, permission, and capacity.
  • Forward, cancel, skip, return, replay, projection, and preference changes.

Accessibility and stability

  • Axe, keyboard, focus transfer, Escape ordering, and touch target checks.
  • Overflow, off-screen focus, false-modal, and hidden-content checks.
  • CLS, long-task, console, page, and request observations.
  • Manual AT review complete or carried as a visible caveat.

Production isolation

  • Preview and production identity remain distinct.
  • No wholesale historical branch merge.
  • Approved delta is reconciled against a clean production baseline.
  • Rollback is defined before production action.
  • Merge and production deployment require separate Founder authorization.
