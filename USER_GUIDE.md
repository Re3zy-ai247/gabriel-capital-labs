# Gabriel Capital Labs: Complete User Guide 📖

## Welcome! Let's Fix Your Credit 🎯

This guide walks you through using Gabriel Capital Labs to dispute inaccuracies on your credit reports.

---

## 📍 Your Journey: 5 Simple Steps

### **Step 1: Sign Up (2 minutes)**
1. Go to the **Sign Up** page
2. Enter your email and create a password
3. Check your email to verify
4. **You're in!** You now have a free account

### **Step 2: Complete Your Profile (5 minutes)**
1. Go to **Settings** from the main menu
2. Add your full name and address
3. This information is used to generate your dispute letters
4. **Save** when done

### **Step 3: Upload Your Reports (10 minutes)**
1. Get your FREE credit reports from: **AnnualCreditReport.com**
   - You're entitled to 1 free report per year from each bureau
   - Download reports from Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion
2. Go to **Upload Reports** in our app
3. Select the files and upload
4. We'll analyze them automatically

### **Step 4: Review Your Inaccuracies (10 minutes)**
1. Go to **Tradelines** to see what we found
2. Each item shows:
   - **Creditor**: Who reported it
   - **Status**: What it says (Open Collection, Charge-Off, etc.)
   - **Bureaus**: Which bureaus report it
   - **Priority**: How much it hurts your score
3. Use the **Strategist** to see which items to dispute first

### **Step 5: Generate Dispute Letters (5 minutes per letter)**
1. Go to **Letters** → **New Letter**
2. Select an inaccuracy to dispute
3. Choose your dispute strategy:
   - **Completeness**: Missing required info
   - **Accuracy**: Wrong amount/status
   - **Verification**: Can't be verified
   - **Originality**: Misrepresentation
4. Click **Generate Letter**
5. Review the letter (edit if needed)
6. Print or download
7. Mail to the bureaus listed in the letter

## 💡 Understanding the 90-Day Journey

After you mail dispute letters, bureaus have **30 days** to investigate. Here's what happens:

**Days 1-30:** Bureaus investigate your dispute
**Days 31-60:** They respond to you and update credit reports
**Days 61-90:** Allow time for bureau systems to update fully

Go to **Journey** tab to track progress across all three bureaus!

---

## 📊 What Does Each Status Mean?

### Account Status
- **Open**: Account still active
- **Closed**: You paid it off or closed it
- **Collection**: Debt sent to collections agency
- **Charge-Off**: Creditor gave up trying to collect

### Dispute Status
- **Drafted**: Letter created, waiting to print
- **Mailed**: You sent the letter to bureaus
- **Investigating**: Bureaus are looking into it (30 days)
- **Responded**: Bureau responded to your dispute
- **Resolved**: Removed or corrected ✓

## ❓ Common Questions

**Q: How long does this take?**
A: 30-90 days total. Most improvements show up within 60 days.

**Q: Will this hurt my credit score?**
A: Actually, disputing inaccuracies can **help** your score when items are removed or corrected.

**Q: Can I dispute the same item twice?**
A: Not recommended. Bureaus can flag repeated disputes. Let the first round complete (30-60 days) before disputing again.

**Q: What if the bureau doesn't respond?**
A: After 30 days with no response, the item should be removed automatically. We'll help you follow up.

**Q: Do you actually send the letters?**
A: No. You print and mail them yourself. This keeps you in control of the process and is actually more credible to bureaus.

**Q: Is this legal?**
A: 100% legal. You have the right to dispute inaccuracies under the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA). We just make it easier.

**Q: Why is this cheaper than credit repair companies?**
A: We automate the process, so you don't need to pay for a service to do what you can do yourself. We handle the hard part (accuracy), you handle the mailing (easy part).

## 🚀 Pro Tips to Maximize Results

### Tip 1: Prioritize High-Impact Items
Use the **Strategist** tab. It shows which disputes will help your score the most. Focus on collections and charge-offs first.

### Tip 2: Dispute One Bureau at a Time
You don't have to dispute all three bureaus simultaneously. Start with one, see results, then dispute the others.

### Tip 3: Keep Records
After you mail each letter, note the date. Track when responses come back. Screenshot the letter before mailing.

### Tip 4: Be Patient
Don't follow up before 30 days have passed. Bureaus have 30 days to investigate. Give them time.

### Tip 5: Upgrade if Generating Many Letters
If you're planning to dispute 10+ items, Premium ($99/month) pays for itself since you'd normally be limited to 3/month.
