# CreditVector — The Founder Walkthrough (Phase -0 merged simulation)

One film, four altitudes, one cast. Merged from the five simulations (SIM-NEW-OPERATOR · SIM-ACTIVE-OPERATOR · SIM-CLIENT-JOURNEY · SIM-AGENCY-OWNER · SIM-SYSTEM-EXPERIENCE — scene references point there; each sim carries the full storyboard). Everything below is grounded in the locked architecture + real product code; `TARGET STATE` marks designed-not-yet-built mechanics; `GAP` marks places the design is silent — finding those is this phase's purpose.

## Act I — Jordan discovers the machine (SIM-NEW-OPERATOR, 24 scenes)
Jordan Avery signs up on Aug 12 and lands in Mission Control's empty state. The first honest discovery is structural: **the onboarding room exists and nothing leads to it** — `/onboarding` is a fully orphaned route (no signup redirect, no nav entry; confirmed by repo-wide grep). Jordan meets Kai instead through the quiet KaiPresence pill (never auto-opens — correct restraint) and the upload flow. The analysis moment lands: three tradelines, each with a KaiWhy evidence panel, and when Jordan asks "can't I just demand deletion under §609?" Kai corrects the myth in exactly the compliance-bar voice. The first Dispute Package flows through the nine-step chain; the **Download path** gives Jordan a first success with zero money risk (print → certified self-mail → "mark mailed" → the §611 clock explained *from receipt*, not mailing). Weeks later Jordan funds the wallet ("a hold, not a charge" — the education works), meets the FINAL REVIEW ritual (four assertions, the irreversibility warning, prominent and honest), and watches the first Send move through acceptance → production → tracking → delivered → receipt archived, with Kai pleased and precise, never manic. Then the waiting period: Kai goes appropriately quiet — but Act I's tracing exposed the phase's sharpest single defect: **the recommendation deadlock.** One lapsed response window Jordan never logs will outrank every future recommendation forever (`pickRecommendation()`'s fixed branch order has no expiry and no escalation). Quiet is allowed; *stale is also allowed.* Scorecard: Confidence 3 · Momentum 3 · Trust 4 · Progress 3 · Clarity 3 · Motivation 4 · **Completion 2**.

## Act II — Meridian on a Tuesday (SIM-ACTIVE-OPERATOR + SIM-AGENCY-OWNER)
Marcus Webb opens Case Memory and the overnight truth is waiting: a delivery, a bureau response, a rejection. Inside any single client workspace the OS is genuinely strong — one recommendation, basis-carrying, honest timelines. Across fourteen clients it fractures: **there is no cross-client "do this first."** The anti-overwhelm engine is architecturally per-workspace; fourteen managed clients are fourteen `User` rows never unioned into one ranked answer (the planning docs deferred this knowingly). The roster's `needsAttention` is FCRA-window-only — blind to wallet and fulfillment state. The rejection arc is the day's best moment: Kai translates the provider failure without the word "Failed" or any vendor name, preserves the package, highlights the address, and the retry mints a new attempt with a fresh hold — the Recovery Engine's dignity is real. The wallet-low moment exposes Act II's structural finding, confirmed independently from both altitudes: **"agency staff" does not exist.** One login resolves the whole agency; Marcus-versus-Danielle authority is Meridian's office policy on Slack, invisible to CreditVector — and the FINAL REVIEW compliance record cannot say *which human* clicked Submit (SIM-AGENCY-OWNER traced this to `WALLET-COMMITMENT-MODEL §9.2` + the declaration-only `STAFF_USER_LIMIT`). Danielle's week adds the owner's view: no agency-altitude Mission Control; the deficit arc (chargeback → clawback → **agency-wide authorization freeze** → cure) is survivable and honestly narrated, but she lives the disclosed blast radius; accounting export is a GAP; Growth Network and Marketplace present honestly as future. Owner verdict: *OS-grade for a single owner and her wallet, page-grade for anything requiring more than one human.* Marcus: Trust 5, **Completion 2** — nothing tells him he's done. Danielle: **Completion 2**.

The entire on-behalf-of money narration has no audience; the only way Elena learns her charge-off died is Marcus's phone call, outside the product. Operator scorecard: Trust 5 · **Completion 2** (the Journey's ninth node loops by design; nothing says "this client is done").

## Act IV — The ring, the seams, the verdict inputs (SIM-SYSTEM-EXPERIENCE, 920 lines)
Walking every room in one session: the shell, the workspace-cookie identity swap, Kai's continuously-enforced voice, and the zero-fabrication law all read as one system — inside rooms. Between rooms the seams show: Mission Control cannot reach a client directly (mandatory Agency pass-through); the Client workspace is a re-scoped Mission Control rather than a distinct place; the Dispute Package chain is the strongest sequence in the product; the Mail Center evolution reads as a real operational workspace; **the Wallet has no room at all** — the deepest seam found: an exhaustively designed mechanism (anchor, ledger, payer, deficit posture) with no destination an operator can visit, so a healthy balance and an unchecked one are indistinguishable. The edge-case chain (manual → send → wallet-low → top-up → rejection → correction → retry → delivery → response → Round 2) holds together with dignity — the Recovery Engine + Kai catalog are the connective tissue working as designed. Thirteen seams ranked; eight bounded recommendations pre-mapped to locked docs.

## The unanimous findings (all five simulations, independently)
1. **Trust is the system's superpower** (4–5/5 everywhere): zero-fabrication, basis-carrying Kai, honest placeholders, receipts. The compliance discipline built for CROA turns out to be the *experience* differentiator.
2. **Completion is the system's missing emotion** (2/5 everywhere): no day-done for Marcus, no client-done for Elena's case, no week-done for Danielle, no arc-done for Jordan. The Journey loops; nothing ever closes with a bow.
3. Three structural absences: **agency-staff identity** (remedy seam exists: the dormant Operator Identity/Organization/Membership layer), **client-facing view** (the on-behalf-of voice's missing audience), **a Wallet room** (designed mechanism, no destination).
4. Two wiring defects found by walking, not auditing: the **recommendation staleness deadlock** and the **orphaned onboarding route**.
5. The verdict shape entering the adversarial review: **OS-grade rooms, page-grade seams** — "a federation of well-governed, individually OS-quality rooms; not yet one Credit Operating System."

## Does it feel like "The Credit Operating System"?
Preliminary (the Opus experience review adjudicates): **Inside any single room, yes — convincingly.** The moment work crosses a room boundary, an altitude boundary (client↔agency), or a person boundary (operator↔client, owner↔staff), the illusion thins to navigation. The gap is not more features — it is finishing the connective tissue the architecture already implies: the Journey as the visible spine in every room, a Wallet destination, altitude-aware Mission Control, completion moments, and the two identity absences. Every one of these lands as a bounded refinement in an existing locked doc; none reopens the architecture.
