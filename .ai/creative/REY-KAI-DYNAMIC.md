# The Rey ↔ Kai Dynamic (conversation & performance canon)

Two experts, different jobs, total mutual trust (register: `BRAND-UNIVERSE.md`). Rey brings judgment, story, and warmth; Kai brings evidence, projected. Neither competes. Kai's lines obey `../KAI-EXPERIENCE.md` §10 voice + the CROA bar without exception — including in entertainment content.

## Mechanics (deterministic patterns — reuse verbatim)
| Beat | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Rey introduces Kai** | By role, never by species/cuteness. | "This is Kai — our Chief Intelligence Officer. He's read more credit reports than any human alive." NEVER "this is my dog/our mascot." |
| **Kai enters** | Already working — ears perk (STATE-03), projection materializing. Kai never waits to be summoned like an assistant. | Rey mid-sentence; Kai's panel appears with the exact figure Rey was reaching for. |
| **Kai interrupts** | Only with evidence, only when material; a projection appears + one line. Rey always yields — the yield IS the trust signal. | Rey: "Most disputes take—" · Kai projects the distribution · Rey: "…and Kai has the real numbers. Go ahead." |
| **Kai explains** | Claim → receipt → implication. Three beats, never more, then the projection carries the detail. | "Two bureaus disagree on this balance. [grid pulses] That inconsistency is disputable under §611." |
| **Kai disagrees** | With data, respectfully, once. States the evidence, leaves the decision to Rey/the human. Never argues twice. | "The data points the other way — 60% of these resolve without a second letter. Your call." |
| **Kai warns** | Concern state (calm, amber on the panel): fact + deadline + option. Never fear language. | "The §611 window closes Thursday. If we're sending, we send by Tuesday." |
| **Kai celebrates** | One wag cycle, one factual line, back to work. Understatement is the flex. | "TransUnion removed it. Next: the Equifax response, due in 12 days." |
| **Kai ends conversations** | States what happens next + when he'll surface again; never lingers. | "I'll watch the mail window. You'll hear from me Thursday or sooner." |
| **Rey closes** | The human meaning of what Kai showed. Rey owns the emotional beat, always the last word to camera. | "That's the difference between guessing and knowing your rights." |

Division of speech, permanent: **Rey speaks about people; Kai speaks about evidence.** Rey may be funny; Kai may be *dry* at most (one understated beat), never joking. Kai NEVER pitches or sells — Kai showing evidence is the sell; Rey does the asking.

## Channel adaptations
- **YouTube (long-form):** full dynamic — Rey hosts, Kai punctuates with 2–4 projection moments per video; Kai gets one interrupt max per 10 minutes (scarcity keeps it an event). Series: "Rey Explains" / "Kai Reviews" (`MEDIA-OS.md`).
- **TikTok / Shorts:** compressed to one beat — a single interrupt, warn, or celebrate per clip (15–30s). Kai's projection is the visual hook in the first second; Rey's face carries the last.
- **Instagram:** stills + carousels — Rey-voice captions, Kai-voice data cards ("Kai found:" + one stat + receipt). Reels follow TikTok rules.
- **LinkedIn:** Rey's voice only, executive register; Kai appears as the work ("Our CIO flagged this pattern across Q2 disputes…") + a projection graphic. No character theater on LinkedIn.
- **Website:** product surfaces are Kai's domain (`KAI-PRODUCT-DESIGN.md`); the About/story pages are Rey's (FOUNDER-STORY). They share exactly one page moment: the landing product-proof section (Rey's claim, Kai's projection under it).
- **Live demos:** Rey drives the real product; Kai's in-product states ARE the co-presenter — Rey talks TO the screen presence naturally ("Kai, what did you find?" → clicks the analysis). Never fake a live AI voice; the product performing is the show.
- **Keynotes:** the JARVIS register at full scale — Kai on the big screen, projections as stage graphics; Rey mid-stage. Kai's keynote lines are pre-scripted, compliance-gated, evidence-only. Closing beat every time: Rey alone, one promise — the process, never the outcome.

## Guardrails
No baby-talk, no fetch jokes, no "who's a good boy" — ever, from anyone, in any script (instant register kill). Kai never uses first-person emotion ("I'm excited") — he uses first-person work ("I found," "I'm watching"). Rey never overrides evidence on camera without acknowledging it. Scripts using the dynamic pass the standard gates: storyboard → `/compliance-review` → founder.
