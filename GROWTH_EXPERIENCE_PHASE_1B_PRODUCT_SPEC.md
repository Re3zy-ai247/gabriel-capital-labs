# CreditVector Growth Experience Phase 1B — Remediated Founder Preview Product Specification

Status: **WORK AUTHORIZED; CONTRACT RATIFICATION PENDING**
Audience: Founder and internal product, architecture, design, compliance, security, accessibility, and QA reviewers

## Product objective

Let the Founder inspect a proposed professional-capability representation contract without creating a participant program, live status, source integration, independent Kai identity, economic meaning, or production surface.

## Reviewer jobs

The Founder must be able to determine:

1. what each pathway means and explicitly does not mean;
2. whether ownership maturity is honest;
3. which exact pathway/fixture pairs are supported, proposed, unresolved, unsupported, or invalid;
4. whether completion, review, correction, appeal, visibility, and evidence remain separate;
5. whether malformed requests and unknown ownership fail closed;
6. whether Kai explains without deciding or acting; and
7. whether the candidate is safe to approve for a separately authorized next gate.

## Required status copy

The experience displays the seven controlling statuses from the canonical contract. It may not use “approved contract,” “accepted,” “permanent,” “ratified,” or “canonical owner” for the Phase 1B contract or an unratified domain.

Required summary wording after evidence exists:

> Work authorized; protected Preview completed; contract ratification pending.

Before protected-Preview evidence exists, the Preview statement must remain `NOT RUN` or `BLOCKED` rather than being projected as complete.

## Pathway presentation

All seven pathways remain selectable for review, but selection does not imply support. Owner-unresolved pathways render the closed state and withhold detailed favorable evidence. Proposed pathways carry a visible `PROPOSED` label. Only the four positively listed Organization-scope boundary examples may say `SUPPORTED_SYNTHETIC_REVIEW`.

Every selection displays its pair-specific rationale in plain language.

## Semantic copy requirements

- “Completion represented” must be followed by “not approval.”
- “Submitted” may not be rendered as accepted, published, verified, or reviewed.
- Correction copy says it challenges source-fact accuracy, provenance, scope, or currency.
- Appeal copy says it challenges a review decision and requires an underlying decision.
- Supported copy says synthetic review support is not authorization.
- Visible copy says review visibility is not public access.
- Reviewable copy says a review surface is not a ratified contract.
- Evidence copy says evidence-present is not verified.
- Qualification copy says qualification is not eligibility.
- Proposed owner copy says the frozen registry does not resolve that owner.
- No fixture may imply credential, certification, expert status, employment outcome, reputation, access, compensation, credit result, or business result.

## Invalid and unsupported behavior

Unknown query keys, duplicate values, partial pairs, malformed types, unknown literals, impossible combinations, and owner-unresolved operational states render one generic closed receipt. The rejected value is never echoed. Invalid input never silently changes to a favorable state.
