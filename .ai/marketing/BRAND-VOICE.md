# Brand Voice (canonical)

**CreditVector™ by Gabriel Capital Labs — "AI-Powered Credit Intelligence Platform."** Positioning: the Bloomberg Terminal for consumer credit. Premium fintech, not late-night credit-repair TV.

## Voice pillars
1. **Intelligence, not hype.** We explain how credit reporting actually works. Authority comes from statutes, data, and receipts — never from promises.
2. **Plain English over jargon.** De-jargoned, punchy, specific (the 2026-06-23 landing refresh is the reference register). Statute citations appear as *evidence*, not decoration.
3. **Honest by design.** "No outcome is guaranteed" is brand identity, not fine print. We say "dispute inaccurate items," never "remove negatives." Unmeasured = "not yet instrumented," never inflated.
4. **Empowering, self-service.** The user is the operator; we're the instrument panel. "Your data, your disputes, your control."

## Language rules (compliance-fused — violations fail `/compliance-review`)
NEVER: guaranteed deletion/score boost · "§609 loophole/magic letter" · "we remove/fix your credit" · "results typical" implications · fear-mongering.
ALWAYS: educational framing ("learn," "understand," "dispute inaccurate") · disclaimers on outcomes ("illustrative, results vary") · attribute news ("the CFPB reports…").

## Kai (product character & executive identity)
Kai — named after the founder's Shiba Inu — is Gabriel Capital Labs' Chief Intelligence Officer character (company-level IP, `../creative/BRAND-UNIVERSE.md`); a trusted executive, never a mascot or pet. Persona and product-voice rules are canonical in `../KAI-EXPERIENCE.md` §10 (helpful · calm · professional · confident · friendly; never sarcastic/robotic/verbose; process language only, per the CROA bar). Marketing may use Kai framing ("Kai found…") under those rules; mascot artwork requires an owner-approved asset (`../ASSET-REGISTRY.md` CV-KAI-001) before any visual use.
