## Preamble

CreditVector exists to help people and organizations build durable capability through trustworthy education, responsible operations, verified evidence, and useful collaboration. The Growth Network shall create opportunity only when opportunity produces real value for the person served, the contributor, the organization, and the wider ecosystem.

The Growth Network is designed not to operate as a recruiting hierarchy, passive-income plan, popularity contest, credit-outcome bounty, or score-to-cash system. Its legal, employment, tax, accounting, securities, and consumer-law characterization remains undetermined pending written specialist analysis. It proposes two equal paths:

- an **Agency Builder** may establish a successful organization and, only under a separately approved program, qualify for a bounded Growth Distribution based on verified organization-level stewardship;
- a **Professional Operator** may build an independent career or business through their own accepted education, mentorship, creation, consulting, or other legitimate value.

Neither path is superior. Neither path is required for the other. Membership, ownership, status, attention, and proximity to another person do not create a right to that person’s earnings.

## Part I — Purpose and value

### Article 1 — Successful organizations, not recruiting

**RATIFIED PRINCIPLE:** CreditVector does not reward recruiting. CreditVector rewards building successful organizations.

**PROPOSED INTERPRETATION:** No invitation, signup, activation, recruit, downline, lineage, depth, team volume, participation/access purchase, subscription, or marginal headcount may unlock eligibility or increase Agency, indirect, override, or residual compensation. This does not prohibit the Article 12 lawful provider’s own accepted-delivery proceeds.

### Article 2 — Contribution over popularity

**RATIFIED PRINCIPLE:** Contribution matters more than popularity.

**PROPOSED INTERPRETATION:** Likes, followers, views, attendance, minutes, posting frequency, reviews, ratings, applause, reach, and public rank are not economic evidence.

### Article 3 — Retention over acquisition

**RATIFIED PRINCIPLE:** Retention matters more than acquisition.

**PROPOSED INTERPRETATION:** Retention means durable value and a fair choice to remain. CreditVector shall not reward blocked exits, suppressed complaints, inactive-seat inflation, paid duration, or manipulated cohort denominators.

### Article 4 — Education over marketing

**RATIFIED PRINCIPLE:** Education matters more than marketing.

**PROPOSED INTERPRETATION:** Accepted usefulness, accuracy, correction quality, accessibility, and maintained relevance may matter. Promotional volume and unsupported claims do not.

### Article 5 — Mentorship over referrals

**RATIFIED PRINCIPLE:** Mentorship matters more than referrals.

**PROPOSED INTERPRETATION:** A mentor may participate only through their own accepted service under a separately approved stream. Membership or proximity creates no right to a mentee’s future earnings, purchases, recruits, organization revenue, or downstream activity; the lawful payee remains relationship-specific.

### Article 6 — Verified evidence over claimed success

**RATIFIED PRINCIPLE:** Verified evidence matters more than claimed success.

**PROPOSED INTERPRETATION:** Every material decision shall identify the authoritative source, policy version, evidence window, reason, reviewer, and appeal path. Self-attestation alone shall not create economic value.

### Article 7 — Ecosystem strength over extraction

**RATIFIED PRINCIPLE:** Growth must strengthen the ecosystem, not extract from it.

**PROPOSED INTERPRETATION:** A metric that rises while customer outcomes, participant dignity, fair exit, security, compliance, or platform durability declines is not growth.

### Article 8 — Direct value before generalized subsidy

The first economic presumption is direct value: a defined buyer or beneficiary receives an accepted product or service from the person who earns from it.

Generalized organization distributions require separate proof of distinct organization-level consideration and causal GCL value. If that proof fails, the program remains non-monetary or is retired.

### Article 9 — Two equal, composable paths

Agency Builder and Professional Operator are equal paths. A participant may be eligible for either, both, or neither under separate agreements and policies. One path may not be a prerequisite, rank, promotion, or feeder for the other.

### Article 10 — Membership does not determine the payee

Organization ownership, membership, role, management authority, or platform status does not automatically transfer an economic claim. The lawful payee and beneficiary are determined per stream by the actual contracting party, worker relationship, assignment rules, informed consent, tax treatment, and applicable law.

## Part II — Prohibited incentives

### Article 11 — No purchase or pay-to-qualify

No participant payment, purchase, subscription, certification, inventory, service, or access fee may be required for—or improve—eligibility, Agency allocation, seller access, fee rate, rank, indirect compensation, override, or residual. This does not prohibit the Article 12 lawful provider’s own accepted-delivery proceeds under a separately approved direct transaction.

A free identity/security account may be operationally necessary under a later approved program. Paid access may not be. A separately disclosed transaction fee may be charged only after an independently purchased bona fide direct sale under a separately reviewed program.

### Article 12 — No downstream economics

No participant may receive indirect, override, lineage, status-based, or downstream value merely because another participant earns, sells, subscribes, recruits, purchases, stays, or builds an organization. This does not prohibit the lawful payee from receiving proceeds for its own independently purchased, bona fide, accepted direct product or service under a separately approved contract and related-party policy. That voluntary purchase may not affect either party’s eligibility, Agency pool, allocation, reputation, rank, fee rate, or right to earn. No lifetime override, team-volume residual, lineage commission, or multi-level attribution is permitted.

### Article 13 — No credit-outcome economics

No reward, fee, qualification, reputation, or distribution may depend on a credit-score change, deletion, removal, dispute count, financing decision, approval, denial, tradeline outcome, or continued consumer payment.

CreditVector remains software-and-education first and shall not promise credit repair, deletions, score improvement, or financing outcomes.

### Article 14 — No score economy

XP, Growth Reputation, Arena status, badges, participation, presence, or any social/learning score may not be purchased, transferred, sold, spent, pledged, converted to cash, or used as an economic multiplier.

### Article 15 — No activity theater

Raw activity is not value. Posts, clicks, sessions, streaks, response speed, time online, attendance, completions, and content volume may not create compensation without separately verified, accepted consideration.

### Article 16 — No double monetization

The default is one underlying fact, one economic use. A direct service, sale, or licensed asset may not also increase an Agency allocation unless a separate organization-level consideration is prospectively defined, disclosed, accepted, independently evidenced, capped, and approved.

## Part III — Economic instruments and policy

### Article 17 — Instrument separation

Qualification, reputation, XP, entitlement, attribution, obligation, payable status, provider instruction, provider acceptance, settlement, tax record, and customer billing are different instruments owned by different contexts.

No implicit conversion exists between them.

### Article 18 — Qualification is not money

Eligible, qualified, pending, held, approved, accrued, payable, scheduled, accepted, settled, reversed, and disputed are distinct states. Only the future authoritative economic journal may record an approved obligation. Only a reconciled provider receipt may prove settlement.

### Article 19 — Shadow means no entitlement

**Qualification shadow** records are non-monetary observations. They shall never display currency, payable units, or personalized earnings estimates.

An **internal financial simulation** may use synthetic or access-restricted hypothetical values solely for Finance stress testing. It is never participant-visible, never a statement or balance, and never promotable.

Neither mode may be promoted, copied, migrated, or reclassified into live obligations.

### Article 20 — Policy before performance

Every economic policy shall be versioned, explainable, effective-dated, published, and frozen before its observation window. Budgets, inputs, denominators, caps, evidence treatment, and appeal rules may not change retroactively.

### Article 21 — Bounded, funded liability

No live Growth Distribution cycle opens without a prospectively approved participant-independent company funding source, maximum liability, accountant-defined approved-obligation backing, reserve taxonomy, and no-cross-subsidy rule. Only verified incremental margin from nonparticipant end-customer transactions—or another counsel-approved participant-independent company source—may inform affordability. Payments by any CGn participant, applicant, economically eligible person, or commonly controlled entity are excluded individually and in aggregate and may never affect whether a pool opens, pool size, qualification, or allocation. The backing amount and instrument require separate specialist approval.

New participant money, required purchases, or later participant activity shall never fund earlier participant returns.

### Article 22 — Honest cadence

The 15th-of-the-month schedule applies only to Agency Growth Distribution after activation. It means scheduled instruction under the ratified calendar rule, not guaranteed bank receipt. Other revenue streams follow their source agreement and settlement maturity.

### Article 23 — No silent forfeiture

Evidence relevance and attribution may expire prospectively under a published policy. An approved obligation shall not silently expire, disappear, or revert to Gabriel Capital Labs. Holds, reversals, settlement, and unclaimed-property treatment require notice, authority, reason, history, and appeal. Voluntary cross-stream offsets and recovery are prohibited; any legally compelled collection occurs under separate authority outside the stream ledgers.

### Article 24 — Earnings truth

CreditVector and its participants shall not make unsubstantiated earnings, lifestyle, passive-income, recurring-income, typicality, or payment-date claims.

Any permitted earnings representation must reflect the relevant population, time period, material conditions, expenses, fees, refunds, and uncertainty. Exceptional results shall not be presented as typical.

## Part IV — Evidence, reputation, privacy, and fairness

### Article 25 — One source owner

Every fact has one canonical owner. Growth Network experiences may reference or project authorized facts but shall not copy Identity, Organization, Membership, Billing, Reputation, Performance Intelligence, Learning, Mentorship, Marketplace, Community, messaging, task, calendar, document, customer, or evidence truth.

### Article 26 — Growth Reputation is a private projection

Growth Reputation shall be a rebuildable, non-scalar projection inside the canonical Reputation boundary. The participant-facing term should be **Growth Contribution Record**.

It is private by default, explains dimensions and source provenance, and creates no public ranking, star rating, leaderboard, cash conversion, or permanent social label.

### Article 27 — Freshness, not score decay

No lifetime scalar Growth Reputation score shall decay. Each evidence dimension may have a prospectively published relevance window. Stale evidence produces “insufficient recent evidence,” not punishment.

### Article 28 — Privacy and dignity

Collect and use only data necessary for the declared purpose. Protected attributes, raw tax identifiers, payment credentials, unrelated customer data, private communications, fine-grained presence, and covert behavioral surveillance shall not influence qualification, reputation, distribution, or worker evaluation.

Access, correction, retention, deletion, audit, minimum-cohort, and appeal rules shall be designed before persistence or participant visibility.

### Article 29 — Human review and appeal

A source-owned integrity signal may open or inform a separately governed case; a Growth Reputation projection may not. No signal may silently punish. Material adverse economic decisions require understandable reasons, access to relevant evidence subject to security/privacy limits, a human reviewer, conflicts disclosure, and an independent appeal.

No opaque AI score shall be the final decision.

### Article 30 — Fair exit

Organizations and participants shall have fair, lawful, non-retaliatory exit paths. Retention metrics may not create pressure to obstruct departure, conceal complaints, keep inactive seats, withhold documents, or manipulate status.

## Part V — Operations, Kai, and governance

### Article 31 — Team Operations is composition, not ownership

Team Operations is an organization-authorized workspace that composes canonical owners. It does not own generic tasks, channels, messages, announcements, meetings, calendars, notes, documents, presence, courses, certifications, customers, or evidence.

Community remains the cross-organization forum. Agency Command remains the owner of its agency queue/routing/assignment/priority/deadline orchestration and is not modified by CGn. Unresolved records require an ownership ADR before schema.

### Article 32 — Kai is intelligence, never authority

Kai may prepare, recommend, coordinate through cited plans, summarize, explain, and educate from authorized read projections.

Kai never owns or autonomously changes money, identity, organizations, membership, customers, calendar, meetings, tasks, campaigns, messages, documents, source evidence, qualification, fraud cases, holds, billing, tax, prices, contracts, ledger, provider instructions, or settlement. A human and the canonical owner execute approved actions.

## Part VI — Interpretation and amendment

### Constitutional priority

When growth conflicts with compliance, trust, participant dignity, security, fair exit, truthful evidence, or funded liability, those protections win.

When a product metric conflicts with a constitutional article, the metric changes; the article is not reinterpreted to preserve the metric.

### Required policy hierarchy

```text
applicable law and binding regulatory obligations
  → CreditVector Constitution and frozen ownership laws
  → this Growth Network Constitution
  → Founder-ratified ADRs
  → effective-dated Growth Policy versions
  → program agreements and operating procedures
  → user experiences, analytics, and Kai explanations
```

A lower layer may be stricter. It may not weaken a higher layer.

### Amendment rule

An amendment requires:

1. a written change showing the exact article and reason;
2. Founder approval;
3. CEO, Engineering, Design/Product, Compliance, Security, and QA review proportional to impact;
4. specialist counsel/accounting/tax/privacy/payment/worker review where applicable;
5. prospective effective date and participant notice where applicable;
6. immutable version history;
7. proof that the change does not introduce recruiting, participation/access-purchase, popularity, credit-outcome, indirect/downstream, or score economics beyond the narrow Article 12 lawful-provider accepted-delivery rule.

No amendment may retroactively reduce an approved obligation or convert a shadow record into money.

### Emergency suspension

A narrowly scoped program or stream may be suspended prospectively for security, fraud, legal, funding, accounting, provider, or reconciliation risk under a named human authority. Emergency suspension:

- does not erase records or approved obligations;
- requires reason, scope, start time, review time, and notice where lawful;
- cannot be executed solely by Kai or an automated model;
- must move to ordinary governance or expire within the ratified period.

### Interpretive tests

Before approving any metric, payment, badge, challenge, campaign, feature, or claim, answer:

1. Would adding a person, participation/access purchase, subscription, or downstream relationship increase someone else’s eligibility, Agency allocation, indirect compensation, override, or residual—other than the Article 12 lawful provider’s own accepted-delivery proceeds?
2. Could the metric rise through popularity, activity volume, blocked exit, credit outcomes, or self-dealing?
3. Is the value independently useful to a buyer, beneficiary, organization, or the ecosystem?
4. Is the source authoritative and independently reviewable?
5. Is the economic use distinct, bounded, funded, explainable, prospective, and appealable?
6. Can the system fail closed without harming an approved obligation?
7. Would the rule remain defensible if every participant understood it and every exceptional result were removed from marketing?

Any “yes” to questions 1–2 or “no” to questions 3–7 requires rejection, redesign, or specialist review.
