# Research — Domain

## Purpose

Evidence gathered from outside the company. Research **supports** the Founder Library; it does not duplicate it.

The distinction is strict: Research holds *what the world shows*. The Founder Library holds *what the company argues*. A Founder Library volume cites Research; it does not absorb it.

## Canonical files

- `dossiers/` — assembled research on a subject, with provenance
- `sources/` — primary sources, retrieved and dated
- `benchmarks/` — measured comparisons
- `papers/` — academic and industry literature
- `notes/` — working notes, explicitly not conclusions

## Generated artifacts

None.

## Ownership

**Domain:** Research. **Owner:** whoever holds research for the subject; Founder by default.

## Rules

1. **Every source records its provenance**: what it is, where it came from, when it was retrieved, and who retrieved it.
2. **A source that was not actually consulted is not a source.** The library's evidence standard forbids citing unread material, and this domain is where that discipline is enforced.
3. Research carries the authority of its sources, never its own (Architecture §5, rank 5).
4. Distinguish evidence from interpretation. Interpretation belongs to whichever domain is arguing.
5. Negative results are retained — a source that disproved an assumption is more valuable than one that confirmed it.

## Version policy

Sources are immutable once recorded — they are dated captures. Dossiers are versioned as they are extended.

## Do not

- **Do not** cite a source here that has not actually been read.
- **Do not** record remembered figures as though verified.
- **Do not** duplicate Founder Library argument here. Cite in the other direction.
- **Do not** delete a source because its conclusion became inconvenient.
