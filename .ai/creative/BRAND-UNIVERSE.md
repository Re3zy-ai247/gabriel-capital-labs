## The world
Gabriel Capital Labs builds **calm intelligence for money-anxious domains**. The universe's emotional signature: walking into a quiet, dark-navy operations room where someone competent is already working on your problem — the opposite of the industry's scream (scam energy, urgency, guarantees). Every GCL product is a room in the same building: same light (INK STUDIO family), same tone (receipts over hype), same staff (Rey and Kai).

## Two permanent characters — the complete cast
- **Rey Gabriel** — founder, visionary, educator. The human. Makes strategic decisions, tells the stories, speaks to people, carries warmth and conviction. Represents the company.
- **Kai** — Chief Intelligence Officer. The intelligence. Never emotional, never arrogant, never sarcastic, never exaggerated; always calm, evidence-first; **projects information, never opinions**. A trusted executive — NOT an assistant, NOT a mascot, NOT a pet. ("Mascot" is retired vocabulary across all docs going forward.)

**The relationship register:** Stark + JARVIS · Batman + Alfred · Jobs + Ive. Two experts with different jobs and total mutual trust. Never owner-and-pet, never human-and-gimmick. Rey never commands Kai like a dog; Kai never performs for Rey. Full mechanics: `REY-KAI-DYNAMIC.md`.

## IP structure
Kai is **Gabriel Capital Labs intellectual property**, designed above the product level. CreditVector is licensee #1. Rules for every current and future licensee product:
1. Kai's identity, emotional range, and hard locks come from `KAI-CHARACTER-BIBLE.md` — one bible for all products, forever.
2. Products may give Kai domain knowledge, never a different personality. Kai in a future product knows different things; he *is* the same executive.
3. Kai's title travels: Chief Intelligence Officer of whatever room he's standing in.
4. New-product Kai integrations require the same Creative-OS gates (scoring, founder approval).
5. Trademark/registration of the character: open counsel item (CLO list).
6. Technical licensing architecture (identity/knowledge/memory/surface separation, license checklist): `../platform/GABRIEL-INTELLIGENCE.md` §2.

## How future products connect
Every future GCL product inherits: the two-character cast · the INK STUDIO visual world · the receipts-over-hype voice · the retrieval-first/AI-last engineering philosophy (Constitution) · the compliance-as-brand posture. A user moving between GCL products should feel like Kai walked into the next room with them — his memory of the user is per-product (privacy law), his character is universal.

## Kai outside CreditVector
Sanctioned company-level appearances (each still pipeline-gated): GCL corporate site/keynotes (`REY-KAI-DYNAMIC.md` §Keynotes) · founder content (Rey's channels — Kai as recurring cast) · future products · press/brand assets. Unsanctioned, permanently: third-party endorsements, meme-coin/crypto associations (hard NO — the Shiba/doge adjacency makes this an active risk to police), political content, any surface that would attach outcome promises to Kai.

## Evolution rules (how the universe grows without drift)
- Characters accrete history, never rewrite it (MCU rule): new series, new rooms, new knowledge — same people.
- Visual refinements happen by bible amendment + regeneration through the gate, never by letting drift accumulate.
- New characters (a third cast member someday) require founder decision + a bible of their own; the two-character dynamic is the default forever.
- Once a year: universe review against this file — what shipped that violates it gets fixed or the bible gets consciously amended (founder-only).

## The decade arc (long-term vision — company level)
CreditVector is **Version 1 of an AI intelligence company**, not the company. If execution succeeds, the decade looks like:
- **Years 1–2 — Prove the room.** CreditVector wins the trust position in consumer credit; Kai becomes the recognizable face of calm financial intelligence; the Brief becomes a citable industry read.
- **Years 3–4 — Adjacent rooms.** The engine (retrieval-first intelligence + compliance-gated content + verified-knowledge libraries) generalizes: business credit & funding readiness, debt navigation, consumer-rights domains beyond FCRA (per `../VISION.md` horizons). Agency OS matures into infrastructure other professionals build on.
- **Years 5–7 — The intelligence layer.** GCL's defensible asset is the verified-knowledge corpus + the trusted character + the distribution: "Kai inside" becomes a mark of evidence-first consumer intelligence; partner/API surfaces license capability the way the marketplace horizon anticipates.
- **Years 8–10 — The institution.** The most recognizable AI company in consumer finance: a media brand (MEDIA-OS at scale), an education institution (attorney-verified library as public good), an infrastructure company (agency/partner rails) — held together by one universe, two characters, and a reputation for never once having promised an outcome.
Anti-goals for the decade: never a data broker, never an advice-without-license product, never a guaranteed-outcome shop (Constitution Art. I; `../VISION.md` §never).
