4. Re-gate, cycle 1

Artifact: COMMITMENT-REGATE.md · NOT READY — foundations sound, one cross-document contradiction restored F4, 10 bounded must-fixes.

The "14 findings" for this pass: a per-defect re-test table covering F3, F4, F5, F6, F7, F9 (6 items), plus 8 new findings (N1–N8). Headline: W1 minted attempt+1 on every re-authorization after a release; W2 mandated "same attempt reused" for the identical event — no value of attempt satisfied both, so a released hold could be silently re-authorized for zero net debit. F4 was restored through the seam between two internally-correct documents. The re-gate's own honesty audit flagged that the prior merge had mislabeled F4 "ELIMINATED," misstated the claim-key grammar as unified when it was three incompatible forms, and wrongly called a Kai-copy join "rename-only" when ~6 of ~19 copy classes had no copy at all. Must-fixes grouped: A — money safety (5 items, blocking), B — correctness (4 items), C — register honesty (1 item).

5. Refinement cycle 2 (pre-decided rulings)

Directive: REFINEMENT-2-DIRECTIVE.md — scope strictly the 10 must-fixes, no redesign, no scope expansion. Four rulings pre-decided by the coordinator:

  • Ruling 1 — unified attempt lifecycle: release always terminal; every re-authorization mints attempt+1, no exceptions.

  • Ruling 4 — FINAL REVIEW sits after the hold, before Submit, bound to a server-issued single-use expiring token.
