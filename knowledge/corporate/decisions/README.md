# Corporate Decisions

## Purpose

Numbered, dated records of what Gabriel Capital Labs has decided. A decision recorded here states the company's position — it is not a technical fact, and it is not a legal conclusion.

## Canonical files

`CD-NNN-<slug>.md` — one file per decision, numbered sequentially and never reused.

Founder ratifications concerning the Founder Library (FR-001, FR-002, FR-003) currently live in that domain's revision log, where they were originally recorded. **They are not duplicated here.** Consolidating decision records into this directory is a future migration, not a copy.

## Generated artifacts

None.

## Ownership

**Domain:** Corporate. **Owner:** Founder; the Board once one exists.

## Rules

1. Every decision records: number, date, authority, scope, what it supersedes, and what it does **not** decide.
2. A decision with engineering consequences is a decision **to be taken through** engineering governance — never one already taken there.
3. Decisions are immutable once ratified. A changed position is a **new** decision superseding the old by number.
4. Numbers are never reused, even for a withdrawn decision.

## Version policy

Immutable. Supersession, not revision.

## Do not

- **Do not** edit a ratified decision.
- **Do not** reuse a decision number.
- **Do not** record a decision here that has not actually been made. A proposal is not a decision.
