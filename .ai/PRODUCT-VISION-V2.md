# Product Vision V2 — the experience CreditVector becomes

Status: CANONICAL experience vision (design, not runtime). Register: closer to Apple/Linear/Stripe/Notion/Arc than to anything called "credit repair." Extends: `KAI-EXPERIENCE.md` (surfaces) · `creative/KAI-PRODUCT-DESIGN.md` (Kai behavior) · `DELIGHT-SYSTEM.md` (moments) · gate: `FOUNDER-STANDARD.md`.

**The invariant — the three-question rule.** At every moment, on every screen, the user can answer: **Where am I? Why? What happens next?** Any screen that fails one of the three is a defect, not a style choice. (This is the product translation of "every feature removes uncertainty.")

## The ideal journey (V2 walk-through)

**1 · First visit / landing.** A quiet, dark, competent room — no countdown timers, no fake urgency, no "DELETE NEGATIVE ITEMS!!" The page proves instead of promises: real UI performing, Kai's cross-bureau projection, one honest sentence ("We promise the process, never the outcome"). *Where: evaluating. Why: asymmetry explained in 20 seconds. Next: one CTA — see your report understood.*

**2 · Upload.** One surface, one action. Drag the PDF; Kai's working states narrate the REAL pipeline stages. No spinner mystery — stage names are the actual steps. Failure is blame-free with the exact fix ("bureau-site PDFs parse best"). *Next: analysis, and the screen says how long it typically takes.*

**3 · First report.** The reveal is the first wow: your report, structured — tradelines with per-bureau presence, plain-English classification, honest scoring. Kai's first insight card: "I found N cross-bureau inconsistencies and M items worth reviewing." Every claim expandable to its evidence. *Where: your file, understood. Next: review the flagged items.*

**4 · First insight → first dispute.** Each flagged item explains itself: what's inconsistent, which statute applies (plain English + citation), what the realistic process is. The letter builder pre-fills strategy + furnisher address; the letter renders grounded-first, refined-second (visibly honest). Print/mail guidance treats the user like an adult. *Next: mail it; the clock starts.*

**5 · The waiting period — where V2 wins.** Legacy products go dead here; CreditVector gets *more* alive: the deadline radar counts the §611 window, the timeline shows the letter in flight, Kai surfaces one education card per week tied to their situation (passive, from the library), the Brief keeps the world moving. Waiting feels like *watched*, not abandoned. *Where: day 12 of 30. Why: bureaus get 30 days by law. Next: response or escalation, and both paths are already explained.*

**6 · Bureau response.** The letter arrives; the user logs/uploads it; Kai's analysis classifies the outcome and — the key beat — **explains what it means and what's next** (verified → MoV option; removed → celebration + what to watch; stall → the escalation path). No response by day 30? Kai already prepared the follow-up options. *Uncertainty at its lifetime peak is met with maximum clarity.*

**7 · Community.** Not a forum bolted on — the place where your situation has company. "Discussions like your dispute" (tag-matched), attorney-verified answers badged, Kai deflecting duplicate questions to existing receipts. Posting feels safe (moderated, compliance-screened).

**8 · Kai interaction (active).** Rare by design and better for it: when the user asks something real, the retrieval waterfall performs visibly (honest theater), the answer arrives with provenance chips and a confidence badge, cost is shown before any paid ask. Every answer leaves the user *more* oriented, never more dependent.

**9 · Victory.** Proportional celebration (one wag, the timeline milestone lighting) + the fact stated plainly + next watch-item. And the quiet ask done right: "Worth telling someone about? — results vary, the process is the product." Referral by trust, not bribe.

**10 · Returning months later.** The re-entry is Kai Home's overnight-delta: what changed, what needs attention, one recommended action. Nothing guilt-trips ("you've been away 47 days!"), nothing resets. The file kept living while they didn't look — that's the retention story. Score log shows the long arc; goals (mortgage-ready) show distance traveled.

## Customer Love principles (deliverable 7 — what "never seen software like this" is made of)
1. **Transparency as interface** — provenance chips, honest pipeline narration, "not yet instrumented" labels. The product shows its work everywhere.
2. **Education over dependency** — every interaction teaches one transferable thing; graduating users is a success metric, not churn.
3. **Professional calm** — Bloomberg-terminal competence with consumer warmth; never infantilizing, never bro-hype.
4. **Feeling watched-over** — the passive layer (events, deadlines, digests) means the product works between sessions; users feel staffed, not sold.
5. **Adult error handling** — blame-free, specific, always a path; support one click away with context attached.
6. **No dark patterns, structurally** — visible credit costs, easy cancellation, celebration without upsell ambush. Trust compounds only if it's never spent.

## Kai's product evolution (deliverable 3 — canon lives in `creative/KAI-PRODUCT-DESIGN.md`; §7 added there)
Summary of the law: Kai teaches through receipts, explains claim→receipt→implication, builds confidence by being visibly right on small things, **disappears when unnecessary** (stillness default; no Kai on focused work surfaces), never overwhelms (one recommendation at a time), and becomes memorable through restraint + the projection signature — not volume.
